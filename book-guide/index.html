A field guide to the

Materials of the Book

❦

or, how to read a book before you read it

Paper, leather, cloth, thread, ink and pictures all changed at known times. Learn what each looks and feels like, and an old book will tell you when it was made, how it was made, and often what it’s worth.

The Blessed Fig Tree · Used and rare books · Antigo, Wisconsin

Hold a page to the light.Lines in the paper mean handmade laid paper, which usually means before 1800.
Feel the type.Letters pressed into the page mean letterpress. A flat print means offset, usually after the 1960s.
Take a loupe to the pictures.Dots mean a photomechanical reproduction. Lines and a plate mark mean an engraving.
