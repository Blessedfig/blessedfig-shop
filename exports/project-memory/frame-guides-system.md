As of 2026-11-22, there are 31 frames. Christian has 15: the original 12 plus Seventh-day Adventist, Jehovah's Witness and Christian Science, grouped by how each tradition describes itself. Other faiths has 11: Orthodox, Conservative and Reform Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh, Bahá'í, Shinto, Wiccan / Pagan and Scientologist. Non-theistic and philosophical has 5: Secular progressive, Secular humanist, Conservative / traditional, Unitarian Universalist and Satanist (non-theistic). Christian asked for Satanist, Scientologist and similar frames, and for Jewish to be split.

Grade rule: S to F = message fit per the tradition's own teaching, including its teaching on art and media. A grade is never lowered by intensity alone. "Depiction concern" (Minor/Discuss/Significant) replaces "Weight". The site's provisional grade maps Minor to A, Discuss to B and Significant to C.

Files: guides in /mnt/project-files/frame-guides/ are generated by frames.py, which keeps the "## Summary" blocks the research thread writes. frames.json and frame-groups.json are the canonical name lists. Old single "Jewish" rows display under all 3 branches, marked "Older combined grade".

**Why:** keeps grading neutral and consistent across threads.
**How to apply:** use the exact names; new frames show "not graded" on older entries until they are re-graded. See [[manga-site-prototype]].
