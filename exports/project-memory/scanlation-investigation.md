Journalistic investigation started 2026-11-22: do fan-translation (scanlation) sites kids use change, soften or add ideology vs the official English release?

Method: ~10 popular series Christian physically owns. Claude builds a per-series "spot list" of ideologically key chapters (confessions, deaths, villain speeches, endings, religion, gender, politics) mapped to volume/page ranges from wikis and summaries. Christian photographs official pages and manually captures the same passages from fan sites. Claude compares and flags changes, plus documents site risks (ads, adult content, malware).

Sourcing rule: fan wikis only locate key chapters, never a source of quoted text (many draw on fan translations). Every quote comes from the official edition or Christian's photos. A wiki quote that differs from official text is itself a lead to note.

**Why:** Christian wants evidence for parents, not assumptions.
**How to apply:** Claude never scrapes or reads scanlation sites itself; they are never a catalog source. Delete scans after analysis, keep short quotes only. See [[store-books-manga-focus]].
