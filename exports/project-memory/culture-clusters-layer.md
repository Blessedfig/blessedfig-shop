Christian (2026-11-22) wants folkways and regional cultural clusters, inspired by Fischer's Albion's Seed; asked whether morality is partly genetic and whether parents can identify their cluster.
Decision: values-only layer. Six 0-10 dimensions (honor vs dignity, order vs freedom, kin vs civic, hierarchy vs equality, tradition vs secular-rational, survival vs self-expression). Clusters: 4 Albion's Seed folkways, Woodard's 11 American Nations, 9 Inglehart-Welzel world zones (Confucian zone gives Japanese-norm context). Parents: 10-question quiz, pick by region, or skip. Never ancestry, race or genetics as input or label (Claude explained why; contested and breaks neutrality).
Update (Christian, 2026-11-22): parents may also enter by self-identified cultural heritage ("Heritage picker" in the spec, 46 options); it only pre-suggests starting clusters with a caveat, multi-select, overridable by the quiz, never framed as genetic. Site has an "Our values" tab with quiz, region pick, skip.
