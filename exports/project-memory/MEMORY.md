# Philosophy of media
Goal: a public-facing website and app (for now) plus database summarizing the philosophy and morality each TV show, book, podcast, movie etc. conveys, tagged by worldview lens. Starting with all manga, English only.
Audience: homeschooling parents and parents of every political, religious or other background, screening what their kids consume or browsing content that matches their ideology. Site stance is strictly neutral; weigh everything evenly, including Christian denominational differences.
Origin: Christian owns a store selling books and manga (stock not yet screened). Store site: theblessedfigtree.com, a static site on GitHub Pages from repo Blessedfig/blessedfig-shop (GitHub connected; repo added to project).
Live guide: https://www.theblessedfigtree.com/manga-guide/ (store menu links to it). Standing permission (Christian, 2026-11-22): threads may merge guide updates to the live site themselves, verifying the page after each merge.
Site: picker across 31 traditions in three groups (Christian 15, other faiths 11 incl. Orthodox/Conservative/Reform Jewish split, non-theistic 5 incl. Satanist and Scientologist); each has a summary guide. Genre tabs. "Our values" tab with folkways/cultural clusters (Albion's Seed 4, Woodard's 11 American Nations, 9 world zones), entered via 10-question quiz, region, or cultural heritage (46 options; suggestion only, never genetic). Adult-tagged titles listed 18+ and hidden by default.
Held titles (Christian, 2026-11-24): show all held titles (sexual content with possible minors) with a warning for now, Blue Exorcist included, rather than hiding them.
Grading (revised 2026-11-22 after Christian's critique that scripture itself would grade poorly): (1) S to F grade = the MESSAGE, judged by each tradition's own documented teaching. (2) Depiction concern column per tradition (Minor/Discuss/Significant). (3) Intensity 0 to 10 for weighing age. "By philosophy" tab ranks closeness S to F.
Analysis: symbolism, situations, language, music tone; which ways of being are portrayed positively/negatively; deeper philosophy essay; "How readers took it"; winners/losers/sympathy; creator and publisher context (documented facts only); family frames; closing full-read checklist.
Lenses: ~30 worldview lenses incl. pragmatism, communitarianism, Confucian ethics; individual vs community scale; political axes.
