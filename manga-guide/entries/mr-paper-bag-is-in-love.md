**Premise.** Umi Asagi, a calm, unflappable university student, is suddenly confessed to by a timid young man who always wears a paper bag over his head; she starts calling him Kamibukuro-kun ("Paper Bag"). He keeps bringing her presents and following her around campus to tell her how he feels. Her best friend sees him as a nuisance and his devotion as stalking, and frightens him into promising to stay away; Paper Bag vanishes, and Umi realizes she had been looking forward to seeing him every day (MyAnimeList synopsis, vol. 1). When Umi later gets into trouble, he comes to her rescue; one reviewer mentions him stopping men who grope her (MyAnimeList review, volume not stated). The friend steps aside once he sees Paper Bag is harmless, and the story becomes a gentle two-person romance; the reason for the bag is explained later (MyAnimeList reviews). His name appears in a user comment as Fuuya (MangaUpdates; to verify).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Can you love someone, and be loved, without showing your face? A young man so shy he lives inside a paper bag confesses to a woman he barely knows. Everyone else sees a weirdo or a stalker; Umi sees someone who is kind to her.

**The answer the story gives.** Yes, if love is sincere and the other person is willing to look past appearances. Umi's calm acceptance and Paper Bag's steady devotion carry the story; the paper bag is a running joke that also stands for the masks shy people wear (MyAnimeList review).

**Characters as arguments.** Paper Bag argues for sincerity over smoothness. Umi argues for judging people by how they treat you. The best friend argues for caution: a man who follows a woman everywhere might be dangerous. The story lets the friend be wrong about this man, which is also why parents may want to talk about the friend's point.

**Symbols and motifs.** The paper bag: anxiety, protection and the face behind it. Gifts: love shown before it can be spoken. Rescue: the timid man is brave when Umi needs him.

**Traditions it echoes.** Beauty and the Beast and other "see the heart, not the face" stories; the Japanese shy-hero romance (the reviewer compares him to masked or bang-covered shy characters in other manga). For faith readers, 1 Samuel 16:7.

**How each lens is portrayed.** The story is a light comedy with no argued philosophy. Its gentle individualism (be accepted as you are) sits beside a traditional courtship.

**Shifts across the series.** Vol. 1: sudden confession, campus comedy, the friend's warning and Paper Bag's disappearance, then a rescue. Vol. 2: the couple grows closer, the reason for the bag, and "a little drama at the end" (MyAnimeList review). Exact chapter placement is not available.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why do you think he wears the paper bag? What "paper bags" do people hide behind in real life?
2. Umi's friend thought Paper Bag was a stalker. Was that a fair worry?
3. What is the difference between showing someone you like them and pressuring them?
4. What did Umi notice about him that others missed?
5. When is it brave to be seen?
