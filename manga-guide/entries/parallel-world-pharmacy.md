**Premise.** Kanji Yakutani, a 31-year-old Japanese pharmacologist driven by the death of his little sister from a brain tumor, works himself to death. He wakes as Farma de Médicis, the ten-year-old second son of the court pharmacist Bruno in the San Fleuve Empire, a medieval-style world where good medicine is for the rich. The god of medicine, Panactheos, has blessed him: he can see illness, create or erase any substance whose structure he knows, and casts no shadow (Wikipedia). He cures Empress Elisabeth of tuberculosis, opens a pharmacy for nobles and commoners alike, starts a cosmetics shop staffed by women pharmacists who lost their jobs after having children, and founds a new dispensing guild when the old guild's master, Veron, sabotages him. An Inquisitor, Salomon, attacks him as an evil spirit, then recognizes the god's mark, tries to kill himself in penance and is saved by Farma's surgery. Then the Black Death arrives from overseas, spread on purpose by Camus de Sade, a pharmacist banished for experiments on living people; Farma, his father and the Diocese fight the outbreak, and Farma destroys Camus and the demonic power possessing him (Wikipedia, anime episodes 1-12; One Peace Books blurbs, vols. 1-7).

## Life message

**Knowledge and skill are gifts to be shared so that no one, rich or poor, dies needlessly; and a second life should be lived with others, not worked away alone.**

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What is knowledge for? Kanji Yakutani knew more than almost anyone about medicine and spent it all on work until it killed him. Reborn in a world where medicine is cruel guesswork and a privilege of the rich, he has the chance to use what he knows differently.

**The answer the story gives.** Knowledge is for sharing, and life is for living. Farma's first act of rebellion is to price medicine for commoners (ep. 2); his second is to prove his claims with a microscope rather than demand trust (ep. 3). The story keeps reminding him that a cure is no use to a healer who works himself to death again (Wikipedia synopsis).

**Characters as arguments.** Bruno argues for tradition that can learn: he attacks his son, then rethinks his whole practice and his parenting (ep. 3). Veron argues for protecting a guild, and his bitterness has a reason (ep. 8); refusing the new cure, he dies of the plague (Wikipedia). Salomon argues for religious caution and turns to gratitude when shown mercy (ep. 7). Camus argues for knowledge without ethics and becomes a bioterrorist (eps. 9-12).

**Symbols and motifs.** The missing shadow, a sign that Farma is something more than a boy (ep. 1). The microscope. The ice dome over a plague village (vol. 6). The pharmacy's accidental name, "Parallel World Pharmacy," which hides the truth in plain sight (ep. 4).

**Traditions it echoes.** The isekai reincarnation story; medical-history drama about germ theory and the end of bloodletting; and, for religious readers, the healer-saints who served the sick without payment. The Diocese's path from Inquisition to partner echoes debates about faith and science, resolved here in cooperation.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Secular humanism and progressivism lead: evidence and access to care win. Utilitarian public-health thinking shapes the plague arc. Religion is treated as a real power in the world that can be wrong and can change.

**Shifts across the series.** Vols. 1-2: rebirth, the Empress's cure, the pharmacy opens. Vols. 3-5: the Church's suspicion, the new guild, family. Vols. 6-8: the Black Death and Camus. Japanese vols. 9-13 were not covered by sources opened; the light novels end with Farma sacrificing his powers to save the empire (Isekai Wiki; spoilers; the manga may differ).

**Discussion questions.**
1. Kanji worked himself to death trying to help people. Was that good or bad?
2. Why did the guild hate Farma's cheap medicine?
3. How does Farma prove something people cannot see?
4. Salomon tried to kill Farma, then tried to kill himself. Why did Farma save him?
5. If you could bring one modern invention to a medieval world, what would help most?
