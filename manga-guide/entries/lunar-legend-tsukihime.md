**Premise.** As a boy, Shiki Tohno nearly died in an accident and woke able to see "death lines" on everything; cutting along them destroys whatever they belong to. A wandering magician, Aoko Aozaki, gave him glasses that block the sight, and his family sent him to live with relatives (Type-Moon wiki; Wikipedia). Eight years later, as a second-year high-school student, he is called back to the old-fashioned Tohno mansion by his younger sister Akiha, now head of the family, with the maids Kohaku and Hisui. On the way home he is seized by a sudden fit and cuts a stranger to pieces; the next day she is alive: Arcueid Brunestud, a "True Ancestor" vampire, who makes him her bodyguard to hunt the vampire behind a string of killings in the town (ANN; AniList). They fight Nrvnqsr Chaos (vol. 2), then the reincarnating vampire Michael Roa Valdamjong, whom the manga makes a far stronger villain than the game (Tropedia). Ciel, a school senior who is secretly an executor of the Church and a former host of Roa, hunts both (AstroNerdBoy). The Tohno family's demon-blooded secret and the tragedy of SHIKI, Akiha's true brother, come to light (Tropedia; ANN vol. 3). Shiki and Arcueid fall in love. After Roa is destroyed, Arcueid leaves to sleep, perhaps forever; in an epilogue added by the manga, Shiki, with Ciel's help, finds her castle and they watch the sunrise together, though readers disagree whether this is real or her dream (Tropedia; AstroNerdBoy; Type-Moon wiki).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What do you do with a power that can only destroy? Shiki sees the lines along which every thing and person will die. The story asks whether such a person can live gently, and whether love is possible between him and a being whose nature is to feed on humans.

**The answer the story gives.** Choice matters more than nature. Shiki uses the death-sight to protect; Arcueid restrains her thirst out of love; Akiha proves, by her horror at violence, that she is not a killer (Tropedia). Those who refuse limits, above all Roa, who keeps being reborn to cheat death, are destroyed.

**Characters as arguments.** Shiki argues that knowing death can make life precious. Arcueid argues that innocence can survive inside a predator. Ciel argues for duty and atonement. Roa is the case against grasping at endless life. SHIKI, the friendly boy overwhelmed by his blood (Tropedia), argues that inheritance can be a tragedy rather than a choice.

**Symbols and motifs.** Glasses that hide the death lines; the moon; blood as inheritance and food; the castle where Arcueid sleeps; the final sunrise (Tropedia).

**Traditions it echoes.** Gothic vampire romance, the Japanese family-curse story, and Nasu's own earlier The Garden of Sinners (the death-perception eyes, Wikipedia). Christian readers may see original-sin echoes in the cursed bloodline.

**How each lens is portrayed.** The occult is the furniture of the world, presented without a verdict. Existential courage is affirmed. Christian institutions appear as hard, effective hunters, neither mocked nor endorsed. Might-makes-right is defeated.

**Shifts across the series.** Vols. 1-2: the killing of Arcueid and the battle with Nrvnqsr Chaos (ANN). Vol. 3: school, the Tohno family history, Ciel's secret (ANN). Middle volumes: Roa's campaign and the family secrets (SHIKI, Akiha). Final volumes: Roa's defeat, Arcueid's farewell, the tragic last chapter and the added reunion epilogue.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Shiki can see how everything ends. How would that change the way you treat people?
2. Arcueid holds back her thirst because she loves Shiki. What does self-control have to do with love?
3. Roa keeps being reborn to escape death. Why is that the villain's wish?
4. The Tohno family hid terrible things. What happens to families that keep secrets?
5. Is the ending real or a dream? Which would you prefer, and why?
