**Premise.** Kokomi Kumamori got her first kiss in kindergarten from a boy named Kyohei Tajima and has wanted a storybook romance ever since. In her second year of high school she meets a classmate named Kyohei Tajima and thinks fate has brought them back together, but he is a different boy with the same name: blunt, dry and dismissive of everything she calls romantic. Yet he is quietly kind, and he asks her to show him what "romantic" means (vol. 1 blurb). Over the summer Kokomi takes a part-time job and meets the real childhood Tajima, called Kyon, who becomes a rival for her (vol. 2). In the final volume a school-festival zombie film, Kyon's serious bid and her growing feelings for the dry Tajima come to a head (vol. 3). The database tags suggest Kokomi and Tajima end as a couple and Kyon's feelings go unreturned (MangaUpdates; likely). A digital side-story volume shows the pair after the ending (vol. 4).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Is love something fate hands you, or something you build by getting to know a person?

**The answer the story gives.** Kokomi's fairy tale points her to the right name and the wrong boy, and then to the real childhood prince; the story favors the boy she actually came to know (likely), whose quiet kindness shows over time (vol. 1-3 blurbs).

**Characters as arguments.** Kokomi is romance and faith in destiny. Tajima is realism that is secretly tender. Kyon is the fairy tale come true, and the story asks whether that is enough.

**Symbols and motifs.** The kindergarten first kiss; a shared name; the zombie film at the school festival (vol. 3 blurb: being with the right person turns the worst into the best).

**Traditions it echoes.** Shojo romance and the "destined first love" trope; the classic contrast of sense and sensibility.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Gentle expressive individualism: honest feelings rewarded; no other worldview is argued.

**Shifts across the series.** Vol. 1 (likely ch. 1-4): meeting and misunderstanding; vol. 2 (ch. 5-8): summer job and the real Kyon; vol. 3 (ch. 9-12): school festival and the ending; vol. 4: after-story and extras.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Kokomi believed in fate because of one kiss. What makes someone the "right" person?
2. Tajima seems rude but is kind. How can you tell what someone is really like?
3. Is it fair to Kyon that he was "first" but not chosen?
4. When does wanting someone to yourself become unhealthy?
