**Premise.** A year after Fairy Tail's final battle, Natsu Dragneel, Lucy Heartfilia, Happy, Gray, Erza, Wendy and Carla take the 100 Years Quest, a century-old job no wizard has completed: on the northern continent of Guiltina, the immortal Dragon Slayer Elefseria asks them to subdue the Five Dragon Gods (Mercphobia, Aldoron, Selene, Viernes and Ignia, the son of Natsu's foster-father Igneel) before they destroy the world (v1). They tame the water god Mercphobia (v1-3); fight their own brainwashed guildmates when a "White Wizard" possessing the newcomer Touka takes over the guild to erase powerful magic (v2-7); save Touka's parallel world Elentear (v8-10); battle the Dragon Slayer guild Diabolos, which later becomes an ally, and kill the hidden sixth god Dogramag (v11-14); and defeat the alchemist guild Gold Owl, born from Viernes's will, with the help of the android-like weapon Athena (v14-17). In the current arc Ignia tries to turn Guiltina's people into dragons and destroy humanity; Natsu is left for dead and wakes with Igneel's spirit inside him (v19-24; ch. 216-223) (Wikipedia, chapter list).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Can friendship stand up to gods? The 100 Years Quest is a job nobody finished in a century, against dragons who rival the worst enemy of the original series. The sequel asks whether Fairy Tail's bonds still work at that scale.

**The answer the story gives.** Yes, and they spread: the quest keeps turning enemies into friends (Mercphobia, Diabolos, Selene, Athena, Faris). The dragons who refuse friendship (Dogramag, Viernes, Ignia) are the ones who must be defeated.

**Characters as arguments.** Natsu argues for friendship that never gives up. Ignia, Igneel's biological son, argues that strength and hatred of humans are a dragon's birthright; he is Natsu's shadow brother. Faris argues for purifying the world of dangerous magic and learns she was used (v8). Athena, a weapon, learns she already has feelings (v15). Selene, selfish and amused, becomes an ally.

**Symbols and motifs.** The guild mark; fire inherited from a father; keys and contracts; dragons as gods; parallel worlds (Elentear, Edolas).

**Traditions it echoes.** The original Fairy Tail, Western fantasy dragons, Japanese shrine and yokai lore, and a rebirth idea in Irene's story. A Christian reader may see love of enemies; a Buddhist reader, rebirth and letting go.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Community is the message. Might makes right is the villains' creed. Magic and spirits are the setting, used without judgment.

**Shifts across the series.** Volumes 1-7: Mercphobia and the White Wizard. Volumes 8-10: Elentear. Volumes 11-14: Diabolos and Dogramag. Volumes 14-17: Gold Owl and Athena. Volumes 18-24 and ch. 216-223: Ignia, Fire & Flame and the dragonization of Guiltina.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Many enemies in this story become friends. What changed them?
2. Ignia and Natsu were both raised in Igneel's shadow. Why did they turn out so differently?
3. Faris believed she was purifying the world. How can someone do harm while thinking they are doing good?
4. Athena was built as a weapon. What does it mean that she chose to feel?
5. The artists draw the women heroes a certain way. Do you notice it, and does it matter?
