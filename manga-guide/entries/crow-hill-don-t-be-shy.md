**Premise.** The town of Karasugaoka is split in two, and each half is guarded against crime by a volunteer "crime prevention" group: VOICE in the east, led by Sou Izuhara (22), and Karasukai ("Crow's Nest") in the west, led by Tetsuji Shinba (24), a handyman with a strong sense of justice. The two leaders clash so often that everyone thinks they are enemies, but Izuhara is secretly in love with Shinba, who stood up for him when he was a bullied loner in high school and inspired him to change his life. His position, his tough reputation and his shyness keep him insulting Shinba instead of telling him. Database tags point to a stalker, a kidnapping and rescue, and an antagonist who drugs someone without consent; readers say the two end up a healthy, happy couple (AniList; MangaUpdates; MyAnimeList; Seven Seas).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Why is it so hard to say what you really feel, especially when everyone expects you to be tough?

**The answer the story gives.** Reputation and pride are walls; honesty takes more courage than any fight. The man who once taught Izuhara to raise his voice (AniList: encourages people to speak up when they cannot fight back) is the one he must finally speak to.

**Characters as arguments.** Izuhara shows how a bullied boy built a strong shell that now traps him. Shinba stands for plain courage and justice. The stalker and kidnapper (per tags) show love turned into control, the opposite of the leads' respectful bond.

**Symbols and motifs.** The divided town (east and west) as the two men's divided hearts; the crows of Karasugaoka; blushing tough guys.

**Traditions it echoes.** Rivals-to-lovers romance and Japanese delinquent comedies; boys' love conventions, which readers say it handles with mutual respect.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Expressive individualism and progressivism are affirmed without argument; community service is the background of both heroes' lives.

**Shifts across the series.** Volume 1 builds the secret crush and misunderstandings; volume 2 (per tags) brings the stalker, kidnapping and rescue and the couple's resolution.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why does Izuhara insult Shinba instead of telling him how he feels?
2. How did one act of kindness in high school change Izuhara's life?
3. What is the difference between protecting someone and controlling them?
