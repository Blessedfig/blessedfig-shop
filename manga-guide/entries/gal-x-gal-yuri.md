**Premise.** Yua Taooka, a bubbly blonde gyaru (a Japanese fashion subculture of bold makeup, dyed hair and trendy clothes), has just been dumped by her boyfriend. Her best friend Reina Watase, a cool, tanned and more mature gyaru, has been single for a while. Both are in their high school's cosmetics club. They decide to date for a one-year trial, just for the fun of it. The first chapter, likely the original one-shot, runs through the whole year; the rest of the first volume fills it in with dates, birthdays, holidays and a school festival where their class runs a maid café, and they end the year still very much in love. Classmates (the twins Akane and Shion, Yua's airheaded childhood friend Kokona Yazawa) accept the couple, and Reina's parents approve and care for Yua. Later chapters move into their third year of high school (Okazu; Wikipedia; VIZ blurb via MangaUpdates).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What if you dated your best friend? Yua and Reina try it for a year, on a whim, and the series asks whether a relationship begun so lightly can become real.

**The answer the story gives.** Yes: the first chapter jumps across the whole year and lands on two girls still in love (Okazu). The rest is the texture of that year: dates, birthdays and festivals, told as comedy.

**Characters as arguments.** Yua argues for wholehearted enthusiasm: she gushes about Reina to anyone. Reina argues for steadiness, reining Yua in. Their friends stand for a world that simply accepts them (Okazu).

**Symbols and motifs.** Nails, makeup and social-media posts as signs of affection; the hashtag in the title, a relationship lived partly in public; the one-year trial as a promise.

**Traditions it echoes.** The school yuri romance, the gyaru comedy, and the older shojo pattern of best friends whose bond turns romantic. Unlike many yuri stories it starts with the couple already together and skips the drama of confession or rejection (Goyavoyage).

**How each lens is portrayed.** Expressive individualism and progressivism are assumed and celebrated; pleasure-seeking talk is comic and balanced by real devotion.

**Shifts across the series.** Ch. 1 covers the whole trial year; ch. 2-11 fill in that year; later chapters (12-31, not covered by sources) move into third year, and AniList tags hint at time skips and college (to verify).

**Discussion questions.**
1. Yua and Reina start dating just to try it. Is that a good way to begin a relationship?
2. What makes their relationship work, according to the story?
3. The story has lots of jokes about sex. Which jokes, if any, felt out of place for characters your age?
4. Why do you think no one in the story objects to the couple? Is that realistic where you live?
5. What does your family believe about dating and romance, and why?
