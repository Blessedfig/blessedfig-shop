**Premise.** In a world where demons have pushed humanity to the edge of extinction, John Celias is a rank-and-file soldier in the army sent against the Demon King. He lives to see the Hero kill the Demon King, and is cut down by enemy remnants moments later. He wakes as a baby in his home village, which demons had destroyed in his first life, and resolves to use his wartime skills and knowledge to change what is coming (vol. 1). As a boy he meets Nakoruru, who in the future becomes a great archmage, and on her invitation enters the magic academy in the royal capital, where an incident tied to his past life involves Faléna, who becomes his partner (vol. 2-3; MangaUpdates tags a contract and a deal with a demon). Training in the Demon Forest, he reunites with Kerkeiro, a best friend he lost in his first life, and must rescue Kerkeiro's sister Tiana, his lover from that life (vol. 4). He fights a giant dragon with Faléna's help and defends the holy capital from a monster horde (vol. 5), joins the army, and faces a demon attack on the royal capital far earlier than in his first life, while the holy city names a Hero (vol. 6). Helping to awaken the Hero, Mors, John finds events turning in unexpected directions as the Demon King's castle appears and the final war begins (vol. 7, the last) (BookWalker volume synopses).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** If you could go back and live again knowing every loss that was coming, what would you owe the people you lost? John, a soldier who was never a hero, gets that chance.

**The answer the story gives.** Use it to protect them. John prepares from infancy, finds the friends he lost, saves those he could not save, and joins the war earlier and stronger (vol. 1-7).

**Characters as arguments.** John argues that ordinary effort and foresight can matter as much as being chosen. Mors, the chosen Hero, stands for destiny. Faléna (to verify) stands for power with a price. Nakoruru shows genius recognized early.

**Symbols and motifs.** The burned village restored; the battlefield where he died; the second childhood; the Demon King's castle appearing at the end.

**Traditions it echoes.** Time-loop stories and Japanese "do-over" web novels (from general knowledge; to verify); the warrior's duty in many traditions.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Community and pragmatism are endorsed; patriotism appears as service to humanity; the occult supplies tools; might serves protection.

**Shifts across the series.** Volume 1 is childhood and preparation; volumes 2-5 are the magic academy, dungeons, the Demon Forest and a dragon; volumes 6-7 are war, the Hero's awakening and the final battle (BookWalker).

**Discussion questions.**
1. If you could relive a year knowing what would happen, what would you change?
2. John was "just a soldier." Why does the story make an ordinary person the hero?
3. Is it right to accept help from a dangerous source to protect people you love?
4. What does John owe the friends he lost in his first life?
5. How does preparing early change what John can do?
