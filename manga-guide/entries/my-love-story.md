**Premise.** Takeo Goda is a huge, kind first-year at Shuei High whom girls find frightening; every girl he likes falls for his handsome best friend and neighbor, Makoto Sunakawa ("Suna"). When Takeo stops a groper harassing Rinko Yamato on a train, she falls for him, and once Suna clears up a misunderstanding they start dating (vol. 1; Wikipedia; VIZ). The earnest couple learn as they go: friends who badmouth Takeo and a judo tournament (vol. 2), getting lost on a mountain (vol. 3), Suna's sister Ai's secret crush on Takeo (vol. 4), their first kiss after half a year (vol. 6) and the birth of Takeo's baby sister (vol. 6), meeting Yamato's family (vol. 8), a pastry chef who tells Takeo to step aside (vols. 9-10), a school trip to Hokkaido (vol. 11) and a manipulative transfer student who targets Suna (vol. 12). When Yamato's family must move to Spain she runs away from home; Takeo goes to bring her back, later breaks up with her when he thinks he is hurting her studies, regrets it, rushes to Spain and proposes; the three friends start college together (vols. 12-13; Wikipedia; VIZ). Vol. 14 adds college side stories and a comic mystery (VIZ).

## Life message

**Kindness, honesty and loyalty matter more than looks, a true friend is worth as much as a sweetheart, and love grows by putting the other person's good first.**

## Creator and publisher context

- Kazune Kawahara (story), born March 11, 1972 in Takikawa, Hokkaido; a long-time *Bessatsu Margaret* author known for *High School Debut*, *Sensei!* (adapted as the film *My Teacher*, 2017), *Aozora Yell* and *A Star Brighter Than the Sun* (Wikipedia). ## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What makes someone worth loving? Every girl Takeo likes chooses his handsome friend, and Takeo accepts it with a shrug (VIZ). The series asks whether looks or character win in the end.

**The answer the story gives.** Character. Yamato falls for Takeo because he saved her and because he is kind, strong and confident, and she never minds his appearance (Wikipedia). Suna, who could have any girl, turns down every one who mocked his friend (Wikipedia). The romance itself is a series of lessons in putting the other first: Takeo lets Yamato go to Spain, then learns that pushing her away for her own good was also a mistake, and crosses the world to say so (vol. 13; Wikipedia).

**Characters as arguments.** Takeo argues for humble, active goodness. Suna argues for loyalty that asks for nothing. Yamato argues that love sees the heart. Ichinose argues that a talented, handsome man is the better match, and loses without being made a villain (Wikipedia). Ai shows a quiet, unspoken love that ends in blessing someone else's happiness (Wikipedia).

**Symbols and motifs.** Yamato's homemade sweets as her language of love (AniList Food); Takeo's huge body as both a joke and a shelter; the train where they meet; the crowd at the proposal (vol. 13).

**Traditions it echoes.** "Beauty and the Beast" with the roles comically reversed, and the shōjo genre it parodies. Christian readers link it to 1 Samuel 16:7 and 1 Corinthians 13 (Beneath the Tangles); Buddhist readers may see metta, and Confucian readers filial care in Takeo's devotion to his family.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Virtue ethics is the frame and is celebrated. Communitarianism appears in friendship and family. Traditionalism is light: an early engagement and classic protector roles, which some progressive readers may want to discuss.

**Shifts across the series.** Vols. 1-5: getting together, first misunderstandings and rivals. Vols. 6-8: a first kiss, a new sibling, families. Vols. 9-11: the pastry chef rival and the school trip. Vols. 12-13: Spain, the breakup, the proposal and graduation. Vol. 14: college side stories.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why do people misjudge Takeo? Have you ever misjudged someone by their looks?
2. Suna turns down girls who were mean about Takeo. Was that fair? What would you have done?
3. Takeo decides to break up with Yamato "for her own good." Why was that a mistake?
4. Yamato runs away rather than move to Spain. What else could she have done?
5. What makes Takeo and Suna's friendship strong?
