**Premise.** Yukari Maki, a second-year honor student who prides herself on honesty, is asked by classmates to keep their delinquent classmate Yusuke Kusaka in line; rumors say he runs with the yakuza (Mangapedia; L'Éclaireur Fnac). When a new rumor about him spreads, she follows him and finds him at a music studio, Studio Cabana, singing a love song; his sad, sweet voice captivates her, the start of a first love that is at first one-sided (Yen Press and MangaDex blurbs). She agrees to keep his secret if he takes over her class duties and cleaning (L'Éclaireur Fnac). Yusuke (16 or 17) is the vocalist of the band the oops!!, whose other members are older: drummer Akane Miyahara (19), bassist and leader Daigo "Dai" Nakagawa (22), who looks out for him, and guitarist Haruki Shirai (19), a university student (AniList; Mangapedia). Vol. 2 (ch. 5-8) flashes back to how a girlfriend of Yusuke's was harassed by other girls and left him, how he withdrew, tired of being wanted for his looks, and how he met Haruki, who overturned his ideas about love (Julsa; Maruzen Junkudo blurb). Mangapedia states that Yusuke has long been in a physical relationship with Haruki, whom he secretly loves, while she does not want to be his partner; Yukari knows this and still does not give up, and Haruki grows jealous as Yusuke wavers. Readers describe a heavy, tangled early tone and a later arc in which Yusuke and Yukari like each other without acting on it (MechaComic reviews).

**Honest, steady care can reach someone who has been hurt and is hiding, and love that treats a person as a whole human being is worth more than a relationship built on looks or on the body alone.**

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What does it take to be seen for who you are? Yusuke is judged by rumor and wanted for his face; Yukari is valued for being useful and correct. In the studio, each sees the other differently.

**The answer the story gives.** Honesty and patience. Yukari keeps showing up; Yusuke's songs say what he cannot. The relationship Yusuke has with Haruki, physical but without commitment, is shown as leaving him empty (Mangapedia), and the story moves him toward a love that is mutual (MechaComic reviews).

**Characters as arguments.** Yukari argues for honesty and steadiness; Yusuke, for the hurt behind a cold surface; Haruki, for love on one's own terms, which the story questions; Dai, for protecting those you love (Julsa).

**Symbols and motifs.** The studio as a hidden room where the truth comes out; the love song Yukari overhears; vol. 2's chapter title "The Day the World Turned White" and the image of a cold, beautiful "snow woman" for Haruki (Julsa; Maruzen Junkudo).

**Traditions it echoes.** The shoujo "good girl and delinquent" romance and the music manga (Julsa compares readers' expectations with Beck). For faith readers it echoes many traditions' teaching that love is more than the body; for secular readers, current talk about grooming and unequal relationships (MangaUpdates tags).

**How each lens is portrayed.** Self-expression is affirmed through music; virtue (honesty, steadfastness) is rewarded; pleasure without commitment is critiqued.

**Shifts across the series.** Vol. 1 (ch. 1-4): rumor, studio, pact. Vol. 2 (ch. 5-8): Yusuke's past and Haruki. Vols. 3-6 (ch. 9-27): the triangle (likely). Later volumes (to ch. 41): a new arc of mutual, unspoken feelings (MechaComic reviews). Ongoing.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why did Yukari refuse to spread the rumor about Yusuke? What did it cost her?
2. Yusuke is tired of people who like him only for his looks. Why does that hurt?
3. Dai says not to date someone you don't want to protect. What do you think he means?
4. Haruki is older than Yusuke. Why does age matter in a relationship between a teen and an adult?
5. Can music say things people can't?
