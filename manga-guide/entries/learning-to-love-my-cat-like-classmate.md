**Premise.** Minato Seno, a shy, socially anxious first-year high school student, is asked by his homeroom teacher to look after the girl at the next desk, Mugi Nekozane, who sleeps through every class and whose face he has never really seen. When he finally works up the courage to wake her, she hugs him on the spot (ch. 1; Manga UP! blurb). Nekozane loves cats, considers them superior to people and shows little interest in humans, but she opens up to Minato; they bond over cats and photography, and their feelings slowly grow (Wikipedia; AniList). Supporting characters include her protective friend Momoka Usagihara, who at first takes Minato for a stalker, his friend Naoto Takada, a photography club member and the owner of a cat cafe (AniList).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** How does a shy person reach another person who seems to want no one? Minato has to wake a classmate who sleeps through every class and prefers cats to people.

**The answer the story gives.** Gently and patiently, the way you would approach a cat: through small kindnesses and shared interests (cats, photography). The surprise hug (ch. 1) shows that the distant girl may be lonelier than she looks.

**Characters as arguments.** Minato argues that small courage counts. Nekozane argues for self-possession and finds that letting one person in is worth it. Momoka argues for caution about boys, and the story lets her be persuaded, not mocked (AniList).

**Symbols and motifs.** Cats as the image of guarded affection; the camera as a way of seeing someone closely; the desk next to yours.

**Traditions it echoes.** The Japanese quiet-romance tradition and the slice-of-life comic born on social media. Religious readers will find kindness to the lonely and to animals; Buddhist readers, metta.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Virtue ethics (courage, kindness) is affirmed in small doses; communitarian warmth and acceptance of individuality are light touches.

**Shifts across the series.** Volumes 1-2 (ch. 1-12) collect the original web comic: meeting and first friendship. From volume 3 (ch. 13 onward) the magazine serialization widens the cast (Momoka, Naoto, the photography club, the cat cafe). Later developments are not described in sources opened.

**Discussion questions.**

2. Why do you think Nekozane prefers cats to people?
