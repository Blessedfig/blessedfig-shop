**Premise.** Tsugumi Harudori, a shy 14-year-old Japanese girl (fan wiki, citing ch. 2), discovers she can turn into a weapon, a halberd, and transfers to the Death Weapon Meister Academy (DWMA) in Death City, Nevada. She joins the NOT class (Normally Overcome Target), for students who only want to control their powers, rather than the elite EAT class that fights evil. She befriends two meisters, the forgetful, strong Meme Tatane and the proud Anya Hepburn, and cannot choose which one will be her partner. Daily life (dorm, part-time jobs at the Deathbucks Cafe, exams, a baseball game, growing pumpkins) runs alongside a mystery: ordinary citizens become sleepless "Traitors" who attack DWMA students. The witch Shaula Gorgon (sister of Soul Eater's Medusa and Arachne) is controlling them with venom. She mind-controls the senior student Eternal Feather into stabbing a bystander and then trying to kill herself (ch. 14-15), and finally possesses Meme, who kills the teacher Sid Barrett (ch. 36-37). Anya turns out to be Princess Anastasia Yngling, guarded by the EAT agents Akane and Clay. On Halloween, Tsugumi and Anya rescue Meme; Tsugumi chooses both of them, and their three-way Soul Resonance, backed by Lord Death and the Death Scythes, defeats Shaula (ch. 40-42). In the final pages Tsugumi brings a fourth partner, leaving her choice open as a joke (fan wiki; Wikipedia).

## Life message

**Ordinary people find courage through friendship: you do not have to be the strongest to protect the people you love, and you may not have to choose between them.**

## Creator and publisher context

- Atsushi Ohkubo created Soul Eater after his first series, B.Ichi; he later wrote Fire Force (this site's Soul Eater entry, from Wikipedia).
- On-record intent (fan wiki, citing Ohkubo's Soul Eater vol. 20 notes): he started Not! to show details of Death City and the DWMA that the main series had no room to explain, in a story that "moves in a different direction"; he placed it one year before Soul Eater's opening (Twitter, cited by the fan wiki).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Who will you stand beside, and must you choose only one? Tsugumi, a weapon who does not want to fight, has two friends who both want her as a partner.

**The answer the story gives.** Friendship is not a contest. When Shaula possesses Meme, Tsugumi and Anya save her not by force but by reminding her of their life together; Tsugumi then chooses both, and the three resonate as one (ch. 40-42).

**Characters as arguments.** Shaula argues that friends make you weak; Meme answers that her fists exist to make the people she loves happy (ch. 42). Anya argues for pride and duty, then chooses loyalty over her family's command (ch. 39). Akane and Clay show elite duty with a hidden mission. Sid argues for stubborn responsibility and pays with his life.

**Symbols and motifs.** The blunt halberd that grows a blade and wings as Tsugumi's resolve grows; sleep and sleeplessness (the Traitors never sleep; Meme sleepwalks); pumpkins grown for Halloween; the bell tower.

**Traditions it echoes.** The school-life spin-off and the moe ensemble (ANN compares it to K-On!); the Western Halloween and Grim Reaper imagery of its parent series; the Gorgon sisters of Greek myth.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Communitarianism and virtue ethics are affirmed; mind control is condemned; the occult is a fantasy backdrop with good and bad witches. Feminist readings find capable heroines undercut by fan service.

**Shifts across the series.** Vols. 1-2: arrival, dorm, jobs, the Traitor mystery and the bazaar attack. Vols. 3-4: "Soul Eater JOT" vignettes of daily life, summer school, and Meme's growing memory problems. Vol. 5: Halloween, Sid's death, the rescue and the battle with Shaula.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why can't Tsugumi choose between Meme and Anya, and is choosing both a good answer?
