**Premise.** Sora, a Japanese teenager, is one of seven students summoned by the king of Elysia to fight the Demon King, but his only skill is "Walking": he never tires on foot. Judged useless, he is paid off and thrown out of the palace. He then discovers that every step earns experience, unlocking skills such as Appraisal, Alchemy and Domestic Magic, and decides to walk the world (AniList; K Manga blurb). In the light novel he works as an adventurer, befriends the elf Chris and her friend Rurika, who are searching for their childhood friends Sera and Eris, sold into slavery after a war; fights off orcs; and is pursued by Elysia's young intelligence agent "Number 13," whom he wins over with food. He registers her as his "special" slave so she can have papers, and she becomes his adopted sister Hikari (LN vols. 1-2). He buys Sera's freedom with money he earns, shelters the runaway Saint Mia from church assassins (she too becomes his nominal slave), and travels on through kingdoms where he learns Elysia repeatedly summons "heroes" and discards them (LN vols. 2-7, TV Tropes recap). The manga's current position in this story is not confirmed.

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What is a person worth if their only gift seems useless? Sora is thrown away by a king who wanted weapons, and discovers that the humblest skill, walking, adds up to everything.

**The answer the story gives.** Keep moving and keep being kind. Small steps become levels; feeding an enemy makes her a sister; spending a reward on a ruined village makes friends for life (LN vol. 2).

**Characters as arguments.** Sora argues for patient growth and kindness. The king argues for using people as tools and falls (LN vol. 7). Hikari, a child agent who chooses loyalty, and Mia, a Saint betrayed by her church, argue that institutions can fail the people they shape.

**Symbols and motifs.** Footsteps and the step counter; the road; slave collars (bondage and, uncomfortably, belonging); food as friendship.

**Traditions it echoes.** The wandering hero (Kino's Journey, mentioned by a MAL reviewer), the "useless skill becomes powerful" isekai, Exodus-like rescue from bondage.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Pragmatism and found family are endorsed; abuse of power is condemned; liberalism is incomplete, since the story frees people one by one while leaving the slave system in place.

**Shifts across the series.** Early chapters (likely ch. 1-30) are slow-life travel and adventurer jobs; later arcs (LN vols. 2-7) add assassinations, slave markets, dungeon expeditions and politics. Manga coverage beyond ch. 82 is not described in sources opened.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Sora was called useless. What made him valuable in the end?
2. Sora uses slave papers to protect Hikari and Mia. Is that right? What else could he have done?
3. Why does feeding Number 13 change her?
4. What small thing could you do every day that would add up over a year?
