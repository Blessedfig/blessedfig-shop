**Premise.** Amane Komi has always been the shortest boy in class and felt inferior for it, until he started dating Asumi Obinata, the tallest girl in the class (MangaUpdates). Obinata is, in the publisher's words, a high-spec tall girl, strong and very athletic, and Komi a timid little guy; they are happy together but keep the relationship secret because they would be embarrassed to be teased about their height difference (Kadokawa; K-Manga). Volume 2 risks discovery on a movie-theater date; volume 3 brings the sports festival, where Obinata is expected to shine; volume 4 brings an autumn park date, a study session and the culture festival, where Obinata plays a ghost so frighteningly in the class haunted house that Komi has to fill in for her, dressed as a girl (K-Manga volume blurbs). Japanese readers describe it as constant sweet flirting in a kind world, with gags about Obinata's strength (Kadokawa reader comments).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** Does it matter what others think of how you look together? Komi has always felt small; Obinata is the tallest in class. They are happy, and still they hide (publisher blurbs).

**The answer the story gives.** Within the relationship, size does not matter: Komi's inferiority fades because Obinata loves him (MangaUpdates). Outside it, the story lets the fear linger as gentle comedy. Whether they go public is still open in the volumes summarized (to verify).

**Characters as arguments.** Komi argues that worth is measured by others' eyes, and is quietly refuted by his girlfriend's love. Obinata argues for confidence and joy in her own strength.

**Symbols and motifs.** Height and the gap between them; the secret; the haunted-house ghost costume that Komi ends up wearing (vol. 4).

**Traditions it echoes.** The "gap" romantic comedy (tall girl, short boy), the established-couple comedy, and for many readers the teaching that people look on outward appearance while what matters is the heart.

**How each lens is portrayed.** Expressive individualism is gentle and affirmed. Gender roles are reversed without comment, which some readers will enjoy and others will barely notice.

**Shifts across the series.** Volume 1 sets up the secret couple; volume 2 the movie date; volume 3 the sports festival; volume 4 autumn dates, a study session and the culture festival (K-Manga blurbs). The series is ongoing.

**Discussion questions.**
1. Why do Komi and Obinata keep their relationship secret? Would you?
2. Komi felt inferior because of his height. What helps people stop comparing themselves?
3. Is it fair to tease people about how tall or short they are?
4. What makes Obinata and Komi a good couple?
5. Talk about what your family believes about dating in high school.
