**Premise.** Misuzu Kanzaki, 22, an office worker and gaming otaku, is dragged by black hands into another world one night. It works like a role-playing game: she can see a status screen and has enormous mana. Meeting a group of knights, she discovers that her modern knowledge of the body, down to cells, lets her diagnose and heal with magic, and she becomes famous as the "Black Healer," known there as Lin (MyAnimeList; AlphaPolis). She searches for a way home and for the mysterious man in her dreams. Reader reviews describe repeated kidnappings, a summoner who wanted to marry her, a king who offered to make her a concubine (she refused), and her meeting with the Demon King, Noche (vol. 5). In the last volume she rescues the "Light Healer" Lilium from a temple, Noche destroys a royal castle, a jealous elf, Viola, attacks and is killed, and Misuzu chooses to stay as Noche's bonded partner, her lifespan extended to thousands of years (BookLive and Mechacomic reader reviews; vol. 7 blurb).

## Philosophy essay

**The central question.** What is an ordinary person's knowledge worth in a world of magic? Misuzu has no warrior skills, only a gamer's instincts and basic science.

**The answer the story gives.** A great deal, when used to care for others. Healing earns her a name and protectors; her knowledge makes her magic precise. In the end she chooses to stay and bind her life to Noche.

**Characters as arguments.** Lin argues for compassion and prudence. The summoner and the king argue that power entitles them to her and lose. Viola shows jealousy destroying itself. Noel shows loyalty.

**Symbols and motifs.** Black (her hair, the "Black Healer," the black hands that took her) set against the "Light Healer"; status screens; names that bind (she names Noche).

**Traditions it echoes.** Women's isekai fiction and the "beauty and the beast" pattern of the feared ruler softened by love (from general knowledge; to verify).

**How each lens is portrayed.** Practical knowledge is celebrated; magic and gods are setting; coercive power is defeated.

**Shifts across the series.** Vols. 1-4: arrival, knights, healing fame, kidnappings. Vol. 5: meeting the Demon King. Vol. 6: to the temple. Vol. 7: Lilium's rescue, Viola's attack and Lin's final choice (AlphaPolis taglines; BookLive).

**Discussion questions.**
1. What did Misuzu know that made her a better healer than others?
2. Why does Lin refuse the king's offer?
3. Is the Demon King a villain? What makes someone good or bad in this story?
4. Would you stay in another world, or go home?
