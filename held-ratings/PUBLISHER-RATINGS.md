# Held titles: official English publisher age ratings

Checked 2026-11-24 for all 31 titles in manga-quick/held/. English publishers come from MangaUpdates. Each rating is the one the publisher states, or a retailer or distributor listing of that edition where the publisher's own page is gone. "None found" means no rating was found online. It does not mean the book is unrated.

## Summary
- **18+ / Mature (9):** Akira (box set only), Domestic Girlfriend, Fox-Colored Jealousy, Mr. Flower Groom, Little Butterfly, Kamiyadori, Say Please, Affair, Sexless Friend
- **16+ / Older Teen (6):** Hybrid Child, Secrecy of the Shivering Night, Amnesia Labyrinth, Sickness Unto Death, Ani-Imo, Dance in the Vampire Bund II
- **13+ / Teen (2):** Mushoku Tensei, Rizelmine (Viz digital)
- **None found (14):** Jagaaan, Onani Master Kurosawa, Bibliomania, Our Everlasting, Invisible Love, Dash!, Pure Heart, Lover's Pledge, Love and Trap, The Severing Crime Edge, The Young Man Yearns for Love, I'm Obsessed With My Little Bro!, Ii Orc no Hi, Monster Petite Panic

## Notes
- Mushoku Tensei and Rizelmine carry teen ratings from their publishers, but they are held because of our own content review, not because of the rating.
- For the Juné/Digital Manga print titles, the publisher's own site is gone. Their ratings are printed on the books, so a copy in hand would settle the "none found" rows.
- Rows that link only to MangaUpdates had no rating anywhere online; the link is the series record that names the publisher.
