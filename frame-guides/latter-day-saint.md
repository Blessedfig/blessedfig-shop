# Latter-day Saint

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God the Eternal Father, His Son Jesus Christ and the Holy Ghost are three distinct beings. Humans lived with God as spirit children before birth; earth life is a test and a chance to gain a body and grow; through Christ's Atonement all will be resurrected, and most will receive a degree of glory, with exaltation in the celestial kingdom for those who keep covenants. Authority rests on scripture (Bible, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, Pearl of Great Price) and continuing revelation through living prophets.

**What it values:** Faith in Jesus Christ, repentance, baptism and temple covenants; family home evening and scripture study; service and missionary work; tithing; self-reliance.

**What it forbids or discourages:** The Word of Wisdom (no alcohol, tobacco, coffee, tea or harmful drugs); the law of chastity; pornography; profanity and taking God's name in vain; Sabbath-breaking; media that are vulgar, immoral or violent.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between a man and a woman, sealed for eternity in the temple; gender is an eternal identity (The Family: A Proclamation to the World); modest dress. Identity is marked by baptism at eight, priesthood, temple worship, missions and ward life.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Members seek "anything virtuous, lovely, or of good report" (13th Article of Faith); For the Strength of Youth asks young people to choose uplifting media. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Members vary in strictness, for example on PG-13 or R films and fantasy magic.

**Own sources:** Book of Mormon; Doctrine and Covenants; Pearl of Great Price; The Family: A Proclamation to the World; For the Strength of Youth; General Handbook; churchofjesuschrist.org.

**Range within the tradition:** The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints; members vary in strictness about media.

**Own sources:** "For the Strength of Youth" (2022); Word of Wisdom (Doctrine and Covenants 89); General Handbook; Gospel Topics essays.

| Authority, parents and rebellion | Honor parents; follow prophets and leaders. |
| Death, afterlife and suicide | Plan of salvation; suicide treated with compassion (Handbook). |
| Lying and deception | Honesty is a core value (Articles of Faith 13). |

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

For the Strength of Youth (2022) asks youth to choose entertainment that invites the Spirit, replacing earlier specific counsel (such as avoiding R-rated films) with principles. Article of Faith 13 seeks what is "virtuous, lovely, or of good report."

Grade (S to F) = how well a series' message fits this tradition's teaching, as this tradition would judge it. It is not lowered by content intensity alone. Depiction concern = how much this tradition's teaching on depiction would raise about what is shown: Minor = little; Discuss = worth talking through; Significant = many families would hold off or read together.
