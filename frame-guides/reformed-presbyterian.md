# Reformed / Presbyterian

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God is sovereign over all things and created the world for His glory; "man's chief end is to glorify God, and to enjoy him forever" (Westminster Shorter Catechism). Humans are fallen; God saves His elect by grace through faith in Christ alone. Believers rise to eternal life; the unbelieving to judgment. Authority rests on Scripture alone, summarized in the Westminster Standards or the Three Forms of Unity and governed by elders (presbyteries).

**What it values:** God's glory in all of life; preaching of the Word; covenant children and family worship; catechism; Lord's Day observance; vocation and cultural engagement.

**What it forbids or discourages:** The Ten Commandments as expounded in the catechisms, including idolatry and images of God (many Reformed hold that depicting Christ breaks the second commandment), blasphemy, Sabbath-breaking, sexual sin, and lying.

**Family, sex and identity:** PCA, OPC and URC hold marriage between one man and one woman and ordain only men; PC(USA) ordains women and permits same-sex marriage. Identity is marked by infant baptism, profession of faith, catechism and Lord's Day worship.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Common grace allows appreciation of good art by anyone; fiction is judged by its worldview and whether it portrays sin truthfully or attractively. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Conservative PCA, OPC, URC and RPCNA (some sing psalms only) to mainline PC(USA).

**Own sources:** The Bible; Westminster Confession of Faith and Larger and Shorter Catechisms; Heidelberg Catechism, Belgic Confession and Canons of Dort; PCA Book of Church Order; PC(USA) Book of Confessions.

**Range within the tradition:** Conservative (PCA, OPC, URC) to mainline (PC(USA)); the Westminster Standards or Three Forms of Unity are the shared references in conservative churches.

**Own sources:** Westminster Confession and Catechisms; Heidelberg Catechism; Kuyper on common grace; Reformed media reviews (e.g. The Gospel Coalition, Ligonier).

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Common grace (Kuyper) lets families value good art by non-Christians. Westminster Larger Catechism Q139 lists "lascivious songs, books, pictures... stage plays" among sins against the seventh commandment. The second commandment (Heidelberg Catechism Q96-98) makes images of God or Christ a concern for many.
