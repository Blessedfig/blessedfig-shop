# Satanist

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** Both main organizations are non-theistic: Satan is a symbol, not a being to worship. The Church of Satan sees the human as an animal and each individual as their own god; there is no afterlife, so life should be lived fully now. The Satanic Temple uses Satan as a symbol of rebellion against arbitrary authority and of reason and compassion. Authority: for the Church of Satan, Anton LaVey's writings; for The Satanic Temple, its Seven Fundamental Tenets, with no claim to divine authority.

**What it values:** Church of Satan: indulgence rather than abstinence, self-preservation, personal responsibility, individualism. The Satanic Temple: compassion and empathy, justice, bodily autonomy, freedom of others, scientific understanding, admitting and correcting mistakes, and pursuit of the noble.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Church of Satan Eleven Rules: harming little children, killing non-human animals except for food or self-defense, and unwanted sexual advances; compulsive behavior. The Satanic Temple: encroaching on others' freedoms and acting against reason.

**Family, sex and identity:** Consenting adults are free in sexual matters; egalitarian and LGBTQ-affirming. Identity is marked by membership, ritual or community events, and in The Satanic Temple, activism for religious pluralism and separation of church and state.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Literature and art, including Milton's Satan as heroic rebel, are valued; occult imagery is aesthetic or psychodramatic; concern is mainly with stereotyped depictions of Satanists. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** The Church of Satan (individualist) and The Satanic Temple (activist) differ and disavow each other; small theistic Satanist groups have no central texts.

**Own sources:** The Satanic Bible (Anton LaVey, 1969); Nine Satanic Statements and Eleven Satanic Rules of the Earth (churchofsatan.com); The Satanic Temple's Seven Fundamental Tenets (thesatanictemple.com).

**Range within the tradition:** Non-theistic Satanism: the Church of Satan (individualist, founded 1966) and The Satanic Temple (activist, founded 2013), which differ. Neither believes in a literal Satan. Theistic Satanists are small and have no central texts.
