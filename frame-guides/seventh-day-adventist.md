# Seventh-day Adventist

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God created the world in six literal days and rested on the seventh; humanity fell and is saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. The dead sleep unconsciously until Christ's soon Second Coming, when the righteous are raised to eternal life; the wicked are ultimately destroyed rather than tormented forever. Authority rests on the Bible as the only rule of faith, with Ellen G. White's writings regarded as an inspired guide, under the General Conference.

**What it values:** The seventh-day Sabbath (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset); health and wholeness of body, mind and spirit; education; mission and service; Bible study; simplicity.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Alcohol, tobacco and drugs; unclean meats (many are vegetarian); spiritualism and occult practice; sexual immorality; media that degrade or excite the passions; secular entertainment on the Sabbath.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between one man and one woman; modesty and simplicity in dress and adornment. Identity is marked by believer's baptism, Sabbath keeping, Adventist schools and Pathfinders, and health practices.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Ellen White warned against fiction that excites the imagination; today many members accept wholesome fiction while some still avoid it, and spiritualism or magic in stories is a common concern. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Conservative to progressive members, especially on women's ordination, fiction and entertainment.

**Own sources:** The Bible; 28 Fundamental Beliefs; Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual; Ellen G. White's writings (The Great Controversy; The Adventist Home; Messages to Young People); General Conference statements (adventist.org).

**Range within the tradition:** Worldwide church under the General Conference; members range from conservative to progressive, especially on women's ordination and media.

**Own sources:** 28 Fundamental Beliefs (esp. #22 Christian Behavior, #26 Death and Resurrection); writings of Ellen G. White; General Conference official statements; Adventist Review.

| Topic | Usual position |
|---|---|
| Alcohol | Abstinence from alcohol is expected of members (health message, Fundamental Belief 22). |
| Tobacco and drugs | Tobacco and recreational drugs not used; health message central. |
| Magic, occult and spirits | Spiritualism strongly rejected; the dead are unconscious until the resurrection, so ghosts or spirits of the dead shown as real are a particular concern. |
| Other religions and gods shown | Christ as Savior; other faiths respected; strong emphasis on Sabbath and end-time prophecy. |
| Violence, war and revenge | Historic noncombatant stance (many serve as medics); concern about glorified violence. |
| Sexuality, nudity and modesty | Chastity and modest dress; sexual content a concern. |
| Same-sex relationships and gender identity | Marriage between one man and one woman (General Conference statements); same-sex romance usually a significant concern. |
| Gender roles and family | Strong family emphasis; women's ordination debated (2015 General Conference vote). |
| Language and blasphemy | Clean, reverent speech; misuse of God's name a concern. |
| Authority, parents and rebellion | Respect for parents and lawful authority; conscience above state on Sabbath and worship. |
| Death, afterlife and suicide | Death is a sleep until the resurrection; no eternal torment; suicide met with pastoral care. |
| Lying and deception | Lying sinful. |

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Fundamental Belief 22 asks members to choose entertainment "of the highest quality" and avoid what is impure. Ellen White warned against fiction and theatre that excite the passions; "by beholding we become changed" (2 Corinthians 3:18) is a common principle.
