# Eastern Orthodox

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God the Holy Trinity created the world good; humanity, fallen, is restored in Jesus Christ, and the goal of life is theosis: union with God by grace. The dead await the general resurrection and final judgment. Authority rests on Holy Tradition, which includes Scripture, the seven Ecumenical Councils, the Church Fathers, the liturgy and the icons, as held by the bishops in apostolic succession.

**What it values:** The Divine Liturgy and the Holy Mysteries (sacraments); prayer (including the Jesus Prayer); fasting; repentance and confession; humility; veneration of icons and saints; almsgiving.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Occult practices and divination; passions such as lust, anger and pride; sexual immorality; blasphemy; entertainment that inflames the passions or mocks the holy.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage is a sacrament between a man and a woman, an icon of Christ and the Church; chastity and modesty; priesthood for men. Identity is marked by baptism with chrismation and communion in infancy, name days, fasting seasons, Pascha (Easter) and icons in the home.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Iconography is theology in image; sacred art follows canons. Secular fiction is judged by whether it guards the heart and nurtures virtue or feeds the passions. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Greek, Russian, Antiochian, OCA, Serbian and other jurisdictions share doctrine; strictness on fasting, media and modern culture varies by parish, monastery and spiritual father.

**Own sources:** The Bible (with the Septuagint); Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed; decrees of the Ecumenical Councils; the Philokalia; St. John Chrysostom (on raising children); Orthodox Church in America and Greek Orthodox Archdiocese statements.

**Range within the tradition:** Jurisdictions (Greek, Russian, Antiochian, OCA and others) share doctrine; pastoral practice varies by priest and parish.

**Own sources:** Holy Scripture and the Church Fathers; Orthodox Study Bible; "The Orthodox Church" (Ware); jurisdiction catechisms; "Bases of the Social Concept" (Russian Orthodox Church, 2000).

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Guarding the senses (nepsis, watchfulness) is a core practice: what one looks at shapes the soul. Holy images are venerated, so mocking icons or sacred things is serious. Fathers such as John Chrysostom warned against the theatre. "Bases of the Social Concept" (2000) asks media to respect moral values.
