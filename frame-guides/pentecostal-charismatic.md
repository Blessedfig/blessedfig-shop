# Pentecostal / Charismatic

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God created the world; salvation through faith in Jesus Christ; believers are empowered by the baptism in the Holy Spirit, often evidenced by speaking in tongues, with spiritual gifts such as healing and prophecy active today. Christ will return; believers will live with God forever and the unsaved face judgment. Authority rests on the Bible, with the Spirit's guidance and pastoral leadership.

**What it values:** Spirit-filled worship, prayer and fasting; healing; evangelism and missions; holiness of life; testimony.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Witchcraft, occult practice and demonic involvement are taken very seriously; sexual immorality; drunkenness and drugs; profanity; worldliness (stricter holiness standards in some churches).

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between one man and one woman; sex within marriage; modesty; women preach in many churches (the Assemblies of God credentials women). Identity is marked by water baptism, Spirit baptism, lively worship and church community.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Because spiritual warfare is real in this view, many families are cautious about magic, demons and the occult in stories even when fictional; uplifting and faith-building media are preferred. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Classical Pentecostal (Assemblies of God, Church of God in Christ, Foursquare) to independent charismatic churches; holiness standards vary.

**Own sources:** The Bible; Assemblies of God Statement of Fundamental Truths and position papers; Church of God in Christ Official Manual; Foursquare Declaration of Faith; International Pentecostal Holiness Church Manual.

**Range within the tradition:** Assemblies of God, Church of God in Christ, Foursquare, independent charismatic churches; holiness standards vary.

**Own sources:** Assemblies of God "16 Fundamental Truths" and position papers; Church of God (Cleveland) practical commitments; Scripture.

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Media can be an "open door" to spiritual influence, so occult and demonic content is weighed more heavily than in many traditions. Holiness standards (modesty, separation from worldliness) vary by church.

Grade (S to F) = how well a series' message fits this tradition's teaching, as this tradition would judge it. It is not lowered by content intensity alone. Depiction concern = how much this tradition's teaching on depiction would raise about what is shown: Minor = little; Discuss = worth talking through; Significant = many families would hold off or read together. Note the internal range when a series falls on a dividing line.
