# Jehovah's Witness

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** Jehovah is the one true God and Creator; Jesus is his Son, the first of his creation, not part of a Trinity. God's Kingdom will soon replace human governments and make the earth a paradise; a "little flock" of 144,000 will rule in heaven, and most faithful people will live forever on earth. There is no immortal soul or hellfire; the dead are unconscious and await resurrection. Authority rests on the Bible (New World Translation), as explained by the Governing Body.

**What it values:** Bible study; Kingdom Hall meetings; preaching from house to house; Christian neutrality in politics and war; moral cleanness; honesty; love among the brotherhood.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Blood transfusions; celebrating birthdays and holidays with pagan origins (Christmas, Easter, Halloween); spiritism, magic and occult themes; violence, immorality and bad associations; idolatry, including flag salutes.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between a man and a woman; the husband as head of the household; sex only within marriage; modest dress. Identity is marked by baptism, field service, meetings and not taking part in holidays and politics.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Members use Bible-trained conscience in entertainment; the organization warns against content featuring spiritism, demons, magic, violence and immorality, and produces its own videos for children. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** One organization with uniform teaching; families differ in how strictly they apply guidance on entertainment.

**Own sources:** New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures; The Watchtower and Awake!; "Keep Yourselves in God's Love"; What Can the Bible Teach Us?; jw.org.

**Range within the tradition:** One worldwide organization guided by the Governing Body; many entertainment choices are left to personal Bible-trained conscience.

**Own sources:** jw.org articles on entertainment, spiritism and violence; "Keep Yourselves in God's Love"; The Watchtower and Awake!; New World Translation.

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

jw.org counsels asking whether entertainment features what God hates, especially violence (Psalm 11:5), immorality and spiritism (Galatians 5:19-21). Many choices are left to conscience, but magic and occult content is widely avoided.
