# Scientologist

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** Each person is a thetan: an immortal spiritual being who has lived many lives and is basically good. The aim is spiritual freedom and full awareness, reached step by step along "the Bridge to Total Freedom." Existence is viewed through the eight dynamics, urges toward survival that run from self to family, groups, humankind, all life, the physical universe, spirit and the Supreme Being. Authority rests on the writings and lectures of L. Ron Hubbard, applied as written ("standard technology") by the Church of Scientology.

**What it values:** Auditing (spiritual counseling) and training; study technology; communication; personal integrity ("what is true for you is what you have observed yourself"); honesty; the ARC triangle (affinity, reality, communication); helping others and improving society.

**What it forbids or discourages:** The Way to Happiness precepts include not harming people of good will, not doing anything illegal, not murdering, not stealing, being trustworthy and taking care of oneself. The church opposes illegal drugs and campaigns against psychiatry and psychiatric drugs.

**Family, sex and identity:** The second dynamic covers sex, family and raising children; Hubbard's code stresses faithfulness to one's sexual partner. Identity is marked by course and auditing progress, church services and naming ceremonies, and the church's community and humanitarian programs.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Hubbard was a prolific fiction writer and praised the artist's role in society; there is no formal content code. Portrayals of psychiatry and of the church itself are areas of concern. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Church of Scientology members; independent ("Free Zone") Scientologists practice outside the church.

**Own sources:** Dianetics: The Modern Science of Mental Health; Scientology: The Fundamentals of Thought; The Way to Happiness; The Creed of the Church of Scientology; scientology.org.

**Range within the tradition:** Church of Scientology International; independent ("Free Zone") Scientologists practice outside it.

**Own sources:** The Way to Happiness (L. Ron Hubbard, 1981); Creed of the Church of Scientology; Code of a Scientologist; Dianetics.
