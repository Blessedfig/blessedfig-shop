# Bahá'í

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** One unknowable God has sent a series of Manifestations (including Abraham, Moses, Krishna, Buddha, Jesus, Muhammad, the Báb and Bahá'u'lláh) in progressive revelation. The purpose of life is to know and worship God and to develop spiritual qualities; the soul continues after death and progresses toward God; heaven and hell are states of nearness or distance. Authority rests on the writings of Bahá'u'lláh, the Báb and 'Abdu'l-Bahá, the interpretations of Shoghi Effendi, and the elected Universal House of Justice.

**What it values:** The oneness of God, religion and humanity; daily obligatory prayer and reading of scripture; the 19-day fast; truthfulness, chastity, service, consultation; equality of women and men; harmony of science and religion; universal education.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Alcohol and drugs; gambling; backbiting; sex outside marriage; partisan politics; prejudice of any kind; magic and occult practice.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between a man and a woman, requiring parental consent; chastity before marriage; modesty and moderation. Identity is marked by Nineteen-Day Feasts, holy days, the fast in March, and service activities such as children's classes and study circles.

**Stories, fiction and art:** The arts are praised as worship when practiced in a spirit of service; entertainment is a matter of conscience guided by chastity, dignity and avoiding prejudice. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** One administratively unified community; individuals differ in personal application of standards in media and culture.

**Own sources:** Kitáb-i-Aqdas; Kitáb-i-Íqán; Hidden Words; Some Answered Questions ('Abdu'l-Bahá); letters of Shoghi Effendi; Universal House of Justice (bahai.org).

**Range within the tradition:** One worldwide community guided by the Universal House of Justice; individual conscience on most entertainment.

**Own sources:** Kitab-i-Aqdas; writings of Baha'u'llah, 'Abdu'l-Baha and Shoghi Effendi ("The Advent of Divine Justice" on a chaste and holy life); Universal House of Justice letters.
