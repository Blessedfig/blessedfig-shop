# Lutheran

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God created all things; humans are sinners justified by grace alone through faith alone in Christ, not by works. Believers look to resurrection and eternal life. Authority rests on Scripture alone, with the Lutheran Confessions (Book of Concord) as its faithful exposition; Word and Sacrament (baptism, Lord's Supper) are the means of grace.

**What it values:** The gospel of forgiveness; infant baptism and the Lord's Supper; vocation (serving God in daily work and family); the Small Catechism at home; hymnody and church music.

**What it forbids or discourages:** The Ten Commandments as explained in Luther's catechisms, including idolatry and witchcraft, misusing God's name, despising parents, murder and hatred, adultery and lust, theft, and gossip or false witness.

**Family, sex and identity:** LCMS and WELS hold marriage as one man and one woman and ordain only men; the ELCA ordains women and permits same-sex marriages. Identity is marked by baptism, confirmation after catechism instruction, the liturgy, hymnody and the church year.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Luther kept images and music in worship; Christian freedom allows wide use of fiction, judged by conscience and the commandments. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Confessional LCMS and WELS versus mainline ELCA, which differ most on sexuality, ordination and biblical interpretation.

**Own sources:** The Bible; Book of Concord (Augsburg Confession, Small and Large Catechisms); Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod and its Commission on Theology and Church Relations; WELS; ELCA social statements.

**Range within the tradition:** LCMS and WELS (confessional) differ from ELCA (mainline).

**Own sources:** Luther's Small and Large Catechism; Book of Concord; LCMS CTCR reports; ELCA social statements.

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Entertainment is generally adiaphora (neither commanded nor forbidden), judged by love and conscience. Luther valued art and music. Confessional (LCMS, WELS) and mainline (ELCA) families differ in strictness.

Grade (S to F) = how well a series' message fits this tradition's teaching, as this tradition would judge it. It is not lowered by content intensity alone. Depiction concern = how much this tradition's teaching on depiction would raise about what is shown: Minor = little; Discuss = worth talking through; Significant = many families would hold off or read together.
