# Christian Science

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God is infinite Mind, Spirit, Life, Truth, Love; God's creation is wholly spiritual and good, and humans are God's spiritual image and likeness. Sin, sickness and death are not part of God's creation and are overcome through understanding God, as Jesus showed. Life continues after what appears as death, as a progressing spiritual existence. Authority rests on the Bible and Mary Baker Eddy's Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures, the church's "pastor".

**What it values:** Prayer-based spiritual healing; daily study of the weekly Bible Lesson; gratitude, purity, love and spiritual understanding; Christian Science practitioners and the Sunday School.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Alcohol and tobacco; reliance on "material" thinking; Mrs. Eddy warned against hypnotism, mesmerism and spiritualism. Members generally rely on prayer rather than medical treatment, as their choice.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage is honored; purity and moral conduct are expected. Identity is marked by church membership, Sunday School until age 20, Wednesday testimony meetings, the Christian Science Monitor and Reading Rooms.

**Stories, fiction and art:** No formal content code; members are encouraged to watch over thought and to avoid fear-inducing or degrading images, while embracing what is good and uplifting. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Members vary in how strictly they apply spiritual healing and in media habits; some independent Christian Scientists practice outside the Mother Church.

**Own sources:** The Bible (King James Version); Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures; Manual of The Mother Church; Prose Works of Mary Baker Eddy; The Christian Science Journal and Sentinel; christianscience.com.

**Range within the tradition:** Church of Christ, Scientist (The Mother Church, Boston) and branch churches; members vary in practice.

**Own sources:** Science and Health with Key to the Scriptures (Mary Baker Eddy); Manual of The Mother Church; Christian Science Journal and Sentinel.

| Topic | Usual position |
|---|---|
| Alcohol | Members abstain from alcohol (Church Manual). |

| Other religions and gods shown | Respect for other faiths; God understood as divine Mind, Spirit, Love. |
| Violence, war and revenge | No formal pacifism; violence seen as a false belief to be overcome. |

| Authority, parents and rebellion | Respect for parents; spiritual self-government emphasized. |
| Death, afterlife and suicide | Life is spiritual and death is ultimately unreal; strong focus on healing. |
| Lying and deception | Honesty valued. |

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Thought shapes experience, so members guard what they dwell on ("Stand porter at the door of thought," Science and Health 392). Depictions of fear, disease and sin are seen as mental influences to be guarded against.

Grade (S to F) = how well a series' message fits this tradition's teaching, as this tradition would judge it. It is not lowered by content intensity alone. Depiction concern = how much this tradition's teaching on depiction would raise about what is shown: Minor = little; Discuss = worth talking through; Significant = many families would hold off or read together.
