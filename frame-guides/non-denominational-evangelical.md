# Non-denominational Evangelical

## Summary

*Draft from general knowledge; verify against the listed sources.*

**Core beliefs:** God created the world; humans are sinners saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, whose death and resurrection bring forgiveness. Believers will live with God forever; those who reject Christ face judgment. Authority rests on the Bible as inspired and without error, with each church's statement of faith and elders or pastors leading.

**What it values:** A personal relationship with Jesus ("born again"); Bible reading and prayer; evangelism and missions; contemporary worship; small groups; serving others.

**What it forbids or discourages:** Sexual immorality and pornography, drunkenness and drug abuse, occult involvement, profanity and taking God's name in vain, and media that celebrate sin.

**Family, sex and identity:** Marriage between one man and one woman; sex within marriage; many churches teach complementary roles, others are egalitarian. Identity is marked by conversion and believer's baptism, church involvement, youth group and Christian music and media.

**Stories, fiction and art:** Stories are judged by whether they honor God; many embrace fantasy (Narnia, Tolkien) while some avoid magic and occult themes. Reviews such as Plugged In are widely used. See [Teaching on art, media and depiction](#teaching-on-art-media-and-depiction).

**Range within the tradition:** Conservative Bible churches to culturally progressive megachurches; strictness on media varies widely.

**Own sources:** The Bible; Lausanne Covenant (1974); National Association of Evangelicals Statement of Faith; Chicago Statement on Biblical Inerrancy; Focus on the Family (Plugged In); each church's statement of faith.

**Range within the tradition:** Very broad: from conservative Bible churches to culturally progressive megachurches.

**Own sources:** Scripture; Lausanne Covenant; NAE statements; Plugged In (Focus on the Family); The Gospel Coalition; Christianity Today reviews.

| Death, afterlife and suicide | Salvation by faith; heaven and hell; suicide handled pastorally. |
| Lying and deception | Lying sinful; discussion of motives encouraged. |

## Teaching on art, media and depiction

Philippians 4:8 and Romans 14 (freedom and conscience) guide choices. Many distinguish depicting sin from endorsing it and speak of "engaging" or "redeeming" culture; others favor separation. Plugged In and Christianity Today reviews are common references.

Grade (S to F) = how well a series' message fits this tradition's teaching, as this tradition would judge it. It is not lowered by content intensity alone. Depiction concern = how much this tradition's teaching on depiction would raise about what is shown: Minor = little; Discuss = worth talking through; Significant = many families would hold off or read together. Note the internal range when a series falls on a dividing line.
