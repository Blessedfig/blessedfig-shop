is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Kazuki Funatsu. It was serialized in Shueisha's  manga magazine Weekly Young Jump from March 2014 to June 2017, with its chapters collected in 14  volumes. In North America, the series is licensed for English release by Seven Seas Entertainment.


== Premise ==
Nishizuru Yakki has the ability to see ghosts, which are mostly harmless. Despite this ability, he had never believed in monsters or yokai, until he meets a girl named Rokka who is a yokai, then meets others.
