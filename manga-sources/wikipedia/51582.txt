is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hiroaki Samura. It was serialized in Kodansha's  manga magazine  from July 2011 to August 2018, until the magazine ceased publication, and later moved to Kodansha's  manga magazine Monthly Shōnen Sirius in November of that same year. Its chapters have been collected in eight  volumes as of September 2026.
