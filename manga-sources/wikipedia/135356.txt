is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by . It was serialized in Kodansha's  manga magazine Weekly Young Magazine from November 2014 to November 2019, with its chapters collected in 22  volumes. A sequel series, titled The Fable: The Second Contact, ran in Weekly Young Magazine from July 2021 to July 2023. A third series, titled The Fable: The Third Secret, began serialization in March 2025. The story follows a young professional hitman who, after orders from his boss, is tasked to live for one year in Osaka as a ordinary citizen, without killing anyone.

A live-action film adaptation premiered in Japan in June 2019 and a sequel premiered in June 2021. An anime television series adaptation produced by Tezuka Productions aired from April to September 2024. A second season is set to premiere in January 2027.

By October 2026, the manga had over 30 million copies in circulation, making it one of the best-selling manga series. In 2017, The Fable won the 41st Kodansha Manga Award for the General category.


== Plot ==
An exceptionally skilled professional killer known as "Fable" is feared by the Japanese underworld, politicians, mobsters, and public figures. One day, his boss orders him to put everything on hold and lead the life of an ordinary citizen with the assistance of a yakuza clan in Osaka. He is also banned from killing anyone for an entire year. Armed with his Nighthawk pistol and given the pseudonym of Akira Sato, Fable must adjust to normal life alongside his assistant, who is ordered to act as his sister and given the pseudonym of Yoko, all while dealing with criminal elements in Osaka that have their own suspicions about his past.

== Reception ==
By January 2021, the manga had eight million copies in circulation; 15 million copies in circulation by March 2022; over 20 million copies in circulation by October 2022; over 25 million copies in circulation by June 2024; and over 30 million copies in circulation by October 2026.

The Fable won the 41st Kodansha Manga Award for the general category in 2017. Alongside Blue Period, the series ranked 14th on Takarajimasha's Kono Manga ga Sugoi! list of best manga of 2020 for male readers. It was picked as a nominee for "Best Comic" at the 51st Angoulême International Comics Festival, held in 2024.
