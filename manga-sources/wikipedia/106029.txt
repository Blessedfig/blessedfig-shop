is a Japanese light novel series written by NAHAaTO and illustrated by Miya Kazumoto. It was originally serialized as a web novel on the online publication platform Shōsetsuka ni Narō from September 2015 to March 2016. Earth Star Entertainment later began publishing it as a light novel under their Earth Star Novel imprint, releasing 12 volumes and a side story volume between August 2016 and August 2019. A manga adaptation illustrated by Urasuke Ayano was serialized on Earth Star Entertainment's Comic Earth Star service from March to August 2017, with its chapters compiled into a single volume. A second manga adaptation illustrated by Shinichiro Nariie began serialization on the same service in August 2018, with six volumes released as of November 2022. An anime television series adaptation produced by Jumondou is set to premiere in 2027.


== Plot ==
Wazu, an adventurer still haunted by the memory of being rejected by his childhood friend Aria, trains for two years in the mountains, hoping to get stronger. During his training, he discovers that he has become an S-Rank adventurer, now strong enough to defeat the Demon Lord. Despite his newfound strength, he realizes that he still has poor luck regarding love, especially regarding his new crush Salona.
