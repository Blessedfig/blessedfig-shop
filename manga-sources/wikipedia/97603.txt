is a Japanese manga series by Kōji Kumeta. It was serialized in Kodansha's  manga Monthly Shōnen Magazine from December 2015 to July 2020, with its chapters collected in twelve  volumes. A 12-episode anime television series adaptation produced by Ajiado aired from April to June 2020. A compilation anime film premiered in July 2021.


== Plot ==
Kakushi Goto makes a living drawing  manga, but he fears this secret could drive a wedge between him and his daughter, Hime. Determined to protect their bond, he vows never to reveal his profession. The story follows their life together in Nakameguro, beginning when Hime is 8 years old and continuing until she turns 18.
