is a Japanese manga series written by Naoki Yamakawa and illustrated by Akinari Nao. It has been serialized in Kodansha's shōnen manga magazine Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine since June 2016. The manga is licensed in North America by Kodansha USA. An anime television series adaptation produced by Maho Film aired from October to December 2020, and a second season aired from July to September 2021.


== Plot ==
Yuusuke Yotsuya, Iu Shindo, and Kusue Hakozaki are transported by a mysterious half-faced creature who tasks them with completing quests for his game within a specific time limit. As a bonus, if they die, they respawn after a quest is complete, if at least one of them survives.

Later, the trio are joined by Yuka Yukitate, Keita Torii and Glenda Carter. after completing missions, they return to their real life until the next quest starts. As a reward for completing quests, the mysterious creature answers any questions they ask. It is revealed that they are playing in countless alternate worlds and alternating sequences..
