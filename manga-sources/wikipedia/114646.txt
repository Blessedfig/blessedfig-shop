is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eri Ejima. It has been serialized in Media Factory's  manga magazine Monthly Comic Flapper since January 2020. The manga is licensed in North America by Seven Seas Entertainment.

The series follows a group of high school girls who secretly play fighting games together at a prestigious academy where video games are forbidden.

A live-action web drama adaptation aired from May to July 2023. An anime television series adaptation produced by Diomedéa aired from July to September 2026. The adaptations feature real life fighting games and brands in place of the original ones created for the manga, most prominently Capcom's Street Fighter series.


== Plot ==
Aya Mitsuki and Mio Yorue are high school students who attend Kuromi Girls' Academy, a prestigious all-girl school where video games are banned. Despite this, they discover that they are both avid fans of fighting games, and begin playing together in secret. The series follows the two as they bond through their rivalry and improve their skills, finding others who share their passion and entering tournaments, all while keeping their activities hidden from the academy's disciplinary committee.
