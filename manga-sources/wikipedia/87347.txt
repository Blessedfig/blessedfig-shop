is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Natsuki Kizu. It was serialized in the bimonthly manga magazine Chéri+ from 2013 to 2023 and collected in nine tankōbon volumes by Shinshokan.

The series follows four students who form an amateur rock band called Given. Its central storyline focuses on guitarist Ritsuka Uenoyama and Mafuyu Sato, a vocalist who joins the band. Other storylines explore the relationships between bassist Haruki Nakayama and drummer Akihiko Kaji.

Given explores romantic relationships, grief and the characters' personal experiences through their involvement in music. The manga has received several adaptations, beginning with an audio drama released in 2016. An 11-episode anime television series aired in 2019 on Fuji TV's Noitamina programming block. It was the first boys' love (BL) anime to be broadcast in the slot. A theatrical anime film followed in 2020, focusing on the relationship between Haruki and Akihiko. A live-action television drama adaptation aired in 2021.

The manga is licensed in North America by SuBLime, a joint imprint of Viz Media and Animate. Crunchyroll distributes the anime television series and film internationally.


== Plot ==
Given follows four main story arcs, each focusing on different members of the band and their relationships. The first follows Ritsuka Uenoyama and Mafuyu Sato as they form the band and develop a romantic relationship. The second focuses on Akihiko Kaji and Haruki Nakayama during the band's preparation for a music competition. The third follows Mafuyu's childhood friends, Hiiragi Kashima and Shizusumi Yagi, as they deal with the aftermath of Yuki Yoshida's death and their feelings for each other. The final arc follows Mafuyu as he considers his future, while Ritsuka works on completing a song based on a demo recorded by Yuki.

== Reception ==
===Manga===
The second volume of Given reached #39 on Oricon, selling 17,484 copies in its second week for a total of 30,308 copies as of February 2016. The third volume reached #37 on Oricon and sold 24,345 copies in its first week. The series ranked #7 in the boys' love category on the digital book service BookLive! in the first half of 2019.
