is a Japanese web novel series written by Izumi. It originally began serialization on the online publication platform Shōsetsuka ni Narō in November 2014. Takarajimasha later began publishing it as a light novel series with illustrations by Achiki in August 2015; four volumes have been released as of March 2017. A manga adaptation illustrated by Mitsuya Otosu began serialization on DeNA's Manga Box web service in June 2020. An anime television series adaptation is set to premiere in 2027.


== Plot ==
Kazuki Hirasawa, a university student, is reincarnated into another world, now finding himself as Harold Stokes, the protagonist of a video game he used to play. Knowing that in the original game, Stokes is a villainous character who is the subject of numerous death flags, he tried to change his fate and avoid doom. However, he realizes that defeating his innate arrogant personality with his kind-hearted nature from the real world is easier said than done.
