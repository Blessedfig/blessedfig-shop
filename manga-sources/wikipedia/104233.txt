is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Haruka Kawachi. It was serialized in Kodansha's  manga magazine Kiss from November 2014 to November 2025, with its chapters collected in 14  volumes. An anime television series adaptation produced by Tezuka Productions is set to premiere in April 2027.


== Synopsis ==
Whenever it rains, Hina dreams about her younger self playing with a boy she's unsure if she's seen before. Later she meets the boy whose name is Takaaki, but unintentionally time travels to Meiji era Japan while being grabbed onto by him. At the same time, Takaaki mistakes her for his fiancée Hinako.
