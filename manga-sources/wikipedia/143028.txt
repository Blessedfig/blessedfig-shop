is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gorō Nori. It began as a hentai doujinshi series in April 2019, with Nori releasing seven volumes as of June 2025. A hentai original video animation (OVA) adaptation of the doujinshi animated by T-Rex was released from August 2021 to August 2023. An all-ages spinoff manga in continuity with the doujinshi began serialization on Takeshobo's Web Comic Gamma Plus service in March 2022, and has been compiled into ten tankōbon volumes as of July 2026.


== Plot ==
Keita Imaizumi, a high school student, currently lives alone. When three gyaru (gal) schoolmates, Reina Hamasaki, Ruri Kurumizaka, and Yukina Sasaki, find out about this, they start visiting his house on a regular basis and hanging out with him. Some time later, he has sex with all three, leading to the three moving in with him. The four aim to continue being close physically, while keeping their relationship secret at school.
