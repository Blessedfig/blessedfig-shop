is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Sukeroku. It was originally published as a one-shot on the Nico Nico Seiga under Fujimi Shobo's DraDra Sharp brand in October 2022. It later began serialization on the same website under the same brand in March 2023.


== Synopsis ==
Every girl Rui Akai has feelings for tend to confess to his childhood friend Mia Aida due to her appearance resembling that of a  manga prince. Mia offers to train Rui to get better at romance by becoming his partner, but finds herself becoming attracted to him.


== Reception ==
By July 2026, the series had over 400,000 copies in circulation.

The series topped Niconico's 2nd Manga General Election in the "Heart-Pounding" Category.
