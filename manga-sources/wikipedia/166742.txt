is a Japanese web manga series written and illustrated by . It was first published as a webtoon under the Line Manga Indies section, and after collaborating with the Margaret editorial team, it was picked up for official serialization through the Line Manga digital service from January 2020 to April 2022. Shueisha published its chapters in 14  volumes from July 2023 to February 2025. 

The series follows Koyuki Hikawa, an introverted high schooler who keeps her distance from others, until three of her schoolmates draw her out and help her enjoy her adolescence to the fullest.

The first season of an anime television series adaptation produced by Studio Kai aired from April to July 2026. The second season premiered in October of the same year.


== Synopsis ==
High school student Koyuki Hikawa has trouble interacting with others, instead preferring to keep her distance and spend her time alone. However, when she meets Minato Amamiya, he suddenly starts trying to close the distance between them.


== Characters ==
===Main===

:A reserved high school student who keeps her distance from most people, except her friend Miki, maintaining a cold exterior that earns her the nickname "The Ice Queen" among classmates. This demeanor stems from past teasing by boys about her short stature. Over time, she grows more open through her friendship with Miki and her relationships with Yota and Minato. Her limited social experience causes her to overthink her words, resulting in awkward moments. Initially intimidated by Minato's outgoing nature, she eventually develops feelings for him, but is heartbroken when he briefly dates Momoka Kuriki, whose emotional abuse echoes her middle school experiences. Despite this, she still tries to help him overcome his fears and express his feelings more openly, even though initially, Minato tries to push her away. When Minato returns and confesses his feelings, she reciprocates.

As Minato learns from his mistakes and grows closer to them, she warms to him. However, this only further hurts his mental state, as he never reciprocates Momoka's feelings. He eventually breaks up with her when he realizes that he is only masking his fear of loneliness. After Koyuki helps him overcome his fears and self-hatred, however, he realizes that he never stopped loving her. He finally confesses his feelings to her, and she happily accepts.

== Reception ==
Before its serialization, The Ramparts of Ice received a special prize at the "Shueisha Shōjo Manga Grand Prix Powered by Line Manga Indies 2018 Summer". It ranked nineteenth in the web category at the seventh Next Manga Awards in 2021. It ranked first in the Comic CMoa Annual Ranking's  manga category in 2022. It ranked second in AnimeJapan's sixth "Most Wanted Anime Adaptation" poll in 2023. It was nominated for the 69th Shogakukan Manga Award in 2023. It ranked fifteenth, along with The JoJoLands, on Takarajimasha's Kono Manga ga Sugoi! list of best manga of 2024 for male readers. It was ranked sixth in the Nationwide Bookstore Employees Recommended Comics of 2024 list. The series won the 2024 Vertical Manga Award in the completed comic category.
