is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Yuki Kawaguchi. It was serialized in Shueisha's  manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from May 2025 to January 2026. As of March 2026, four volumes have been released.


== Reception ==
Joshua Fox of Screen Rant, reviewing the first chapter, said Otr of the Flame could be a "massive hit", praising the art and writing.
