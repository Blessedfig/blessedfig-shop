is a Japanese  manga series written and illustrated by Yuhta Nishio. It was serialized in Shogakukan's   manga magazine from March 2015 to August 2017, when the magazine ceased its publication, and the series finished in its third and last compiled  volume released in January 2018. The manga has been licensed in North America by Viz Media.


== Plot ==
After being invited to a club by a friend,  finds herself unable to fit in. Her friend is nowhere to be found, and she is soon troubled by unwanted attention. She is rescued by a woman named , known as . The two connect instantly, and their night of conversation culminates in Emi visiting Kei's apartment, where their relationship becomes physical. Emi is initially confused by the sudden intimacy with Kei. However, through this new connection, she is inadvertently drawn into Kei's world of music and invited to participate as a Video Jockey (VJ) for a club event Kei is involved with. This encounter serves as Emi's introduction to a new life and a romantic relationship.
