is a Japanese web novel series written by Yassan Ishino. It began serialization on Kadokawa Corporation's Kakuyomu website in June 2022. A manga adaptation illustrated by Makoto Kuon began serialization on Kadokawa Corporation's KadoComi manga website in December 2022. A light novel adaptation began publication under Enterbrain's Famitsu Bunko imprint in April 2024.


== Synopsis ==
Ceres is a young man who in his previous reincarnation was a Japanese man in his 40s. Ceres becomes a member of his childhood friend Zect's Heroes Party, but is later removed from the party. After being kicked out, Ceres spots Zect's mother Shizuko being sold into slavery, and buys her. Ceres has long held feelings for Shizuko since his youth, and decides to ask her if she could form a party with him. His party later adds the mothers of the members of his former party.
