is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Kōta Kawae. It began serialization in Shueisha's Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine in May 2023. As of November 2026, seventeen  volumes have been released. An anime television series adaptation produced by Studio Hibari is set to premiere in Q3 2027.


== Plot ==
Ever since he was young, Gakuro Yajima has been able to see spirits, but is timid around them due to one malevolent spirit killing his father. One day, he meets Nue, a powerful spirit in the form of a young woman who lives in a secret room in his high school. After an event with a violent spirit causes Gakuro to be mortally wounded, he makes a contract with Nue where she lends him her power, but only on the condition that he help her vanquish evil spirits and work alongside local exorcists who are wary of her.


== Characters ==
===Sixth Elimination Squad===
The squad was created right after Nue revealed her true form, actual name, and the story behind Hyo to the rest of the Captains of the Elimination squads during a meeting. Gakuro Yajima was chosen to lead the squad due to his relationship with Nue, serving as her medium. At first, Nue suggested naming the squad "Origin," but Heiichi quickly rejected the name. Thus, the Sixth Elimination Squad came into existence.

== Reception ==
The series was ranked eleventh in the Nationwide Bookstores Employees' Recommended Comics of 2024 list. The series ranked seventh in the print category at the tenth Next Manga Awards in 2024. The series topped AnimeJapan's "Manga We Want to See Animated" poll in 2025.

Popular authors such as Ryōgo Narita of Durarara!!, Yuuichirou Higashide of Fate/Apocrypha and Tappei Nagatsuki of Re:Zero have praised the series on social media even Kinoko Nasu best known for making iconic stories like The Garden of Sinners,Fate/Stay Night and Witch on the Holy Night is a big fan of the series. Manga artists such as Yukinobu Tatsu of Dandadan , Takuma Yokota of Destroy All Humans. They Can't Be Regenerated. and Shiro Usazaki of Ichi the Witch fame have created fanart of the series.

Jonathan Greenall of Comic Book Resources praised the story, artwork, and characters, as well as the character's designs. Rowan Grover of Multiversity Comics liked the artwork, though  he criticized the humor and fan service, ultimately describing it as "forgettable to annoying".

By July 2025, the series had over 1.2 million copies in circulation. By October 2026, it had over 1.6 million copies in circulation.
