is a Japanese fantasy novel series written by Yoshiki Tanaka. It consists of sixteen novels published from 1986 to 2017. The story and setting are inspired by the history and mythology of ancient Iran. It follows Arslan, the Prince of Pars, as he battles opposing forces following his father's disappearance. The first half of the novel focuses on Arslan as a young prince, while the second half features him as a young adult king who is still dealing with threats to his people.

It was adapted into an anime film duology and a four-episode original video animation (OVA) series released from 1991 to 1995. It was also adapted into a manga series by , which ran in Kadokawa Shoten's Monthly Asuka Fantasy DX from 1991 to 1996. A second manga adaptation, illustrated by Hiromu Arakawa, began in Kodansha's Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine in 2013, which was later adapted into an anime television series in 2015, followed by a second season in 2016.


== Plot ==

The series is set in a legendary amalgamation of ancient Persia and other nearby countries over the course of more than a thousand years. It takes place in a world where magic exists. The story follows the exploits of Arslan, the crown prince of Pars. It is divided into two parts.

In the first part, the neighboring nation of Lusitania takes over Pars after Arslan's father, King Andragoras III, falls victim to a treacherous plot led by some of his most trusted advisors. After barely escaping with his life, Arslan reunites with his loyal servant, Daryun. With only a few more companions backing him up, including the philosopher and tactician Narsus, his young servant Elam, the aloof and cold priestess Farangis, and the traveling musician and con man Gieve, Arslan stands against overwhelming odds to assemble an army strong enough to liberate his nation from the Lusitanian army. The Lusitanian army is led by the elusive warrior known as "Silvermask", who is later revealed to be another contender for the throne of Pars.

In the second part, Arslan, now king of Pars, must defend his country against several external threats, including Silvermask, who is still at large and seeking to claim the throne for himself. At the same time, he must address the needs and hopes of his subjects. During the war, Daryun fought Silvermask, who died in the process. Without Silvermask, the Snake King Zahhāk becomes the Kingdom of Pars's final enemy. However, Daryun dies in combat with the new enemy, prompting Arslan to seek revenge and kill the threat. Arslan is seriously wounded in combat and uses his last bit of strength to tell his people to bury him next to Daryun. After the battle, Pars, Misr, and Maryam perish, leaving Sindhura's kingdom, led by Rajendra, as the main survivor. While the survivors grieve the deaths of King Arslan and his followers, Rostam becomes the successor to the sword Rukhnabad. In the epilogue, Elam dies of old age and meets the spirits of Arslan and his people.
