is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Anji Matono. It was serialized in Shogakukan's  manga magazine Shōnen Sunday S and on the  website from December 2020 to March 2025; its chapters were collected in  volumes. The series follows a young boy recounting ghost stories to an entity, as evil events start to occur. The series is licensed for English release in North America by Seven Seas Entertainment and in Southeast Asia by Shogakukan Asia.


== Plot ==
The story centers on , a young elementary school boy who loses his mother and is subjected to severe abuse and neglect by his foster parents. Driven to the edge of despair, Yuuma tries to jump to his death from his school classroom window. His classmate Hina stops him and stalls for time by telling him about the traditional Japanese Hyakumonogatari Kaidankai (One Hundred Ghost Stories) ritual—the belief that once a person tells 100 ghost stories, actual ghosts and spirits will manifest. Finding a twisted sense of hope and a reason to keep going day by day, Yuuma begins narrating a dark, standalone horror or psychological story in each chapter as he counts his way up to 100.

Each chapter (or cluster of chapters) features a unique, grim, and visceral horror tale—ranging from supernatural specters and cursed objects to human cruelty and bizarre urban legend-style fates. Interspersed between the horrific tales are brief framing segments where Yuuma speaks directly to an unseen presence or entity.


== Reception ==
The manga was nominated for the Next Manga Award's web category in 2022 and 2023.
