Never eat a wild mushroom unless an experienced identifier has checked it in person. Some deadly mushrooms grow in the same woods. Learn with the Wisconsin Mycological Society. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

Flavor

Delicate and sweet, with a texture and taste often compared to crab or lobster.

In the kitchen

  • Torn into pieces and seared until golden
  • "Crab" cakes
  • Butter-basted like scallops

Preparing it

Pick white, fresh ones; yellowing means old and bitter. Cook thoroughly.

Keeping it: Cook, then freeze.

Be careful

  • Expert ID in person.

Typically Aug 19 to Sep 26 around Antigo; as early as Aug 8 in a warm year and Aug 29 in a late one. South slopes run early; frost pockets, cedar swamps and north slopes run late.

Where

On wounds and logs of maple, birch, beech and hemlock.
