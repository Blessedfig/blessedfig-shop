Flavor

Sweet and buttery, smaller than store filberts. Roasting deepens the flavor.

In the kitchen

  • Roast and eat
  • Grind into flour or butter
  • Praline and brittle
  • Toss into salads and granola

Preparing it

Pick husked clusters in late summer, before the squirrels do. Dry the clusters for a week or two, then strip the husks and crack the nuts.

Keeping it: In the shell, cool and dry, they keep for months.

Be careful

  • Beaked hazel husks are covered in fine bristles that irritate skin. Wear gloves.
