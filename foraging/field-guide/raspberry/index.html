Flavor

Bright, floral and tart-sweet, more aromatic than any store raspberry.

In the kitchen

  • Fresh with cream
  • Jam, sauce, shrub or cordial
  • Folded into muffins and scones

Preparing it

Ripe berries slip off the core with no tug. Pick into shallow containers so they do not crush.

Keeping it: They last a day or two at most. Freeze on a tray the same day, or make jam.

Be careful

  • Thorny canes and plenty of ticks: long sleeves and a tick check after.

Where
