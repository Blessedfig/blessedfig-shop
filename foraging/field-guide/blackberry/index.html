Blackberries

Rubus allegheniensis

Flavor

Deep, winey sweetness with a tart edge and crunchy seeds. Shiny black ones are often still sour; wait until they turn a little dull and fall off at a touch.

In the kitchen

  • Cobbler, crisp and pie
  • Seedless jam or jelly (sieve the pulp)
  • Syrup for pancakes and drinks

Preparing it

Pick only berries that release easily. Rinse gently just before eating.

Keeping it: Freeze on a tray, or cook down into jam or juice.

Be careful

  • Thorns and ticks. Recent logging cuts are prime blackberry country.

Where

Sunny cuts, edges and old fields, often on sandier ground.
