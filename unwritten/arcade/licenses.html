The games at Respawn

Every game here is free software, served as part of The Unwritten without changes except where noted. Each keeps its own license.

Doom (Freedoom: Phase 1)

Levels, art and sound: Freedoom, BSD 3-clause, by the contributors to the Freedoom project — freedoom.github.io (license: freedoom/LICENSE.txt; the game file is stored deflated in freedoom/freedoom1.json and unpacked in the browser). Engine: PrBoom (libretro core), GPL-2.0 — source: github.com/EmulatorJS/libretro-prboom. Player: EmulatorJS 4.2.3, GPL-3.0 — source: github.com/EmulatorJS/EmulatorJS (license: ejs/LICENSE.txt; ejs/emulator.min.js is its source files joined, unminified).

Racer

An OutRun-style driving game by Jake Gordon, MIT license — github.com/jakesgordon/javascript-racer (racer/LICENSE.txt). The final version (v4) is used; the Ogg music file is left out.

Hextris

By Logan Engstrom, Garrett Finucane, Noah Moroze and Michael Yang, GPL-3.0 — github.com/Hextris/hextris (hextris/LICENSE.md). Changed for this arcade: Google Analytics, an outside ad script (in index.html and js/main.js), a score-reporting call and the share buttons were removed, and the Exo 2 font is loaded from the game's own folder.

2048

By Gabriele Cirulli, MIT license — github.com/gabrielecirulli/2048 (2048/LICENSE.txt). The iOS start-up images are left out.

The emulators

Console games run in EmulatorJS 4.2.3 (GPL-3.0, above) with these libretro cores, built by the EmulatorJS project (npm packages @emulatorjs/core-* 4.2.3) and served unchanged from ejs/cores/:

  • FCEUmm (NES), GPL-2.0: github.com/libretro/libretro-fceumm
  • Gambatte (Game Boy, Game Boy Color), GPL-2.0: github.com/libretro/gambatte-libretro
  • mGBA (Game Boy Advance), MPL-2.0: github.com/libretro/mgba
  • Stella 2014 (Atari 2600, for your own files), GPL-2.0: github.com/libretro/stella2014-libretro
  • Beetle PCE (PC Engine, for your own files), GPL-2.0: github.com/libretro/beetle-pce-libretro

Changed for this arcade: the emulator never contacts emulatorjs.org (its update check and its download-from-their-server fallback are switched off in ejs/emulator.min.js). Game files a player opens with "Your own game" are read in their browser and never uploaded.
