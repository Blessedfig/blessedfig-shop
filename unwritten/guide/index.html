How to play

The Unwritten is an early, unfinished build. Things change week to week.

Move the cursor and press A to ride straight to that spot or place. C (ZOOM) zooms, M (PLACES) lists places, L (LAYER) shows soils, glacial deposits, elevation or population, and B goes back.

Saving

  • There is one save, kept in this browser on this device. Starting a NEW GAME replaces it.
  • It saves itself often: at doors, after fights and shopping, and when you close the menu.
  • You can also use SAVE in the menu, press C in Explore, or choose Rest upstairs at the Fig Tree counter (heals and saves).
  • Clearing browser data, or playing in a private window, erases your save.

Battles (Story)

Where: the FICTION shelves in The Blessed Fig Tree (six genres with four chapters each; the last chapter is a boss), the heavy bag at UMA (practice), and the Battle Lab.

Party: you start alone. Christian, at the Fig Tree counter, joins first, and others join around town. Three fight at a time, and the rest wait at the Fig Tree.

Turns: each fighter's time gauge fills based on speed, and a full gauge opens your commands. Left and right switch between ready fighters.

Command Does
STRIKE Basic hit. Press A as it lands for GOOD or GREAT
Own command (PLAY, NONFICTION…) Special skills
UNDERSTAND See below
COMBO Team move; everyone in it needs a full gauge
ITEM Use an item
GUARD Half damage until your next turn
LEAVE Try to escape (never works on bosses)

Some skills ask for input: mash A, enter arrows, press A on the beat, or hold and release A. Follow the prompt. When you're hit, press A as the hit lands to BLOCK.

Understanding commands cost Clarity (like magic points) and never miss:

Command Lv Cost Effect
QUESTION 1 Free Mind damage
LISTEN 1 Free +3 Clarity; foe's next hit is softer
NAME IT 1 3 Strong damage; ends DREAD
REFLECT 1 2 Heals you
STEP BACK 4 2 Hard to hit for 2 turns; clears ABSORBED, HOOKED
CITE SOURCE 7 3 Damage, removes a foe boost, reveals a weakness
READ ALOUD 7 4 Hits all foes; clears FOGGED, CLICHED
HOLD BOTH 10 6 Heavy damage; breaks guards

LISTEN, REFLECT and STEP BACK only do damage to foes that are weak to them.

Weak spots: a weakness does 1.5× damage, and a resisted command does half. Some foes heal from a command ("IT LIKES THAT"). Hitting a weakness wears down the foe's guard. When the guard hits zero, the foe is OPEN: it skips its next turn and takes extra damage.

Freed, not defeated: at 0 HP a foe "lets go." If the last hit used a weakness, the foe was OPEN, or Christian gave it the right book, it's Understood and pays 50% more Understanding, which is your experience. Each level-up lets you pick a stat boost.

Down, not out: a member at 0 HP is DAZED, and a Friend's Letter brings them back. If the whole party goes down, you wake up healed and nothing is lost.

Beginner tips

  1. Find weaknesses first (CITE SOURCE, Christian's SHELF TALKER), then hit them until the foe is OPEN.
  2. Low on Clarity? Use QUESTION and LISTEN, which are free. Peppermints and Lavender Sachets restore Clarity.
  3. Practice timing on UMA's heavy bag, or set timed presses to ASSIST.
  4. Rest upstairs before a last chapter, since you can't leave boss fights.
  5. Put fragile members in the back row. They take half as much physical damage, but they also deal half.
  6. Too hard? Set CONFIG to WAIT and GENTLE.

Cheat sheet

Early build. The game is a big work in progress, and things change week to week.

Controls

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD (phone: on-screen pad)
  • A (talk, look, confirm): Enter, Z or Space
  • B (back): Esc, X or Backspace. Hold B to run.
  • Menu: C or Q (phone: the small middle button; gamepad: Start)
  • Town map: M or Tab. Move the cursor, then press A to ride there.
  • Bag: I
  • Doors: walk into them, or face them and press A.

Saving

  • Menu (C), then SAVE, any time.
  • It also saves at every door, after every battle, and when you rest. Saves stay in this browser; choose CONTINUE on the title screen.

Healing

  • The Blessed Fig Tree: talk to Christian at the counter, then Rest upstairs (full heal and a save).
  • Aspirus Langlade Hospital, main lobby: talk to the nurse, then Rest a minute (full heal).
  • Antigo Family Chiropractic Center: an adjustment heals half.
  • Losing a fight costs nothing. Everyone gets back up at full health.

Items

  • You start with $25, 3 Fig Cookies and 2 Friend's Letters (these revive someone, and no shop sells them).
  • Fig Tree SNACKS shelf (right wall, past the counter): Peppermint $1, Fig Cookie $2, Mug of Cocoa $4.50, Grilled Cheese $8, Picnic Thermos $15.
  • Fig Tree APOTHECARY (right wall, further back): Peppermint $1, Chamomile Tea $3 (clears all statuses), Lavender Sachet $6.
  • Gear: Christian, then Bookmarks and gear (stand-in stock, mostly $35+).
  • Boss drops: the last chapter of every shelf always drops gear that no shop sells.
  • Need cash? Pampered Paws Pet Grooming pays you to wash dogs.

Walkthrough

  1. Title screen. Choose NEW GAME.
  2. "Who are you?" Set PLAY to THE STORY. (EXPLORE TOWN lets you walk the town with no battles.) Type a NAME, pick a LOOK, and set STORY to Standard (teen and up) or Mature. Then choose BEGIN.
  3. Your room. You start out facing the screen. Press A on things to look at them if you like. The door is behind you: walk down three steps onto it to go outside.
  4. Fifth Avenue. You come out on the sidewalk facing west. Walk left along Fifth Avenue and cross Clermont Street. The Blessed Fig Tree is the corner shop, and its door is on the corner. Stand on the sidewalk below the door and walk up into it. If someone is blocking the sidewalk, keep walking into them and they'll step aside.
  5. Meet Christian. Walk up to the counter, face it and press A. Pick Come along? and Christian joins. The store is now your home base for resting, shopping and choosing your team.
  6. Make it easy (optional). Menu (C), CONFIG: set CHALLENGE: GENTLE (heals everyone after every fight) and TIMED PRESSES: ASSIST.
  7. Get Danny (recommended). Walk west along the same side of Fifth to Unified Martial Arts and walk up into the door. Talk to Danny at the front desk and pick Train with us? The heavy bags on the mats are a practice fight for learning timed presses.
  8. Your first battle. For now, fights start from the store's shelves (this will change as the story is built). Back at the Fig Tree, check the bookshelves until one says FICTION. The nearest is on the left wall, just above the potted fig tree by the front window. Pick MYSTERY. - A gauge fills for each fighter. When it's full, a command menu opens. - Press A just as a hit lands for extra damage. Press A as a hit comes at you to block. - At level 2, Christian's SHELF TALKER shows a foe's weaknesses and the section it needs. - LEAVE gets you out of most fights, but not the last chapter of a shelf. - COMBO shows up when two partners who know a combo both have full gauges.
  9. Work through the shelf. Each genre has 3 chapters, then a boss in the last chapter. Win one and the next chapter opens. When you finish a genre, it shows (read). Each level-up lets you pick a bonus. Rest upstairs between chapters.
  10. Recruit everyone else (any order). Use the map (M): move the cursor onto a building until its name shows, then press A to ride there.
    • Cecil: Respawn is across Fifth from where you first came outside. Walk down into its door.
    • Daniel: the Palace is on the south side of Fifth, west of Clermont.
    • Vox: the library is just north of Fifth on Clermont.
    • Robin: face Antigo High School from outside and press A.
    • City Administrator: City Hall, 700 Edison St.

  11. City Administrator's summons (optional). Some come with levels. Others unlock when you visit places:

    • face the Langlade County Public Safety Building and press A
    • Public Works on W Pierce Ave, or the Antigo Waterworks on 1st Ave
    • any city park or cemetery
    • the library
    • press A while facing Spring Brook

    Unlock all six and reach level 12 to get MUTUAL AID. 12. Suggested shelf order (easiest first): Mystery, Romance, then Dark Romance or Fantasy, then Science Fiction, then Horror. 13. The end of the current build: all six genres read (24 battles) and all seven companions recruited. After that, the BATTLE LAB in the menu replays any fight at any level, and puts your party back exactly as it was afterward.

That's as far as the build goes right now — more is on the way.
